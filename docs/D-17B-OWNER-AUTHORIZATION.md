**§7 — Competing counter-flow families.** [At minimum: linear; proportional; mean-reverting

recoverability, runaway control, interpretability, agency, save complexity, exploitability,
robustness, long-horizon behavior, waiting cost, maintenance-spam.]

**§8 — Publicity as a decision, not a chore.** "The player should sometimes rationally choose:
PUBLICIZE NOW and sometimes rationally choose: DO NOT PUBLICIZE. A healthy studio should not
need to maintain the button continuously. A distressed studio should be able to use publicity as
ONE recovery lever, not a guaranteed rescue."

**§15 — Anti-spam gate (mandatory).** "A policy that simply uses Publicity whenever legally
available must NOT dominate sane strategic policies. Likewise: Never Publicize must not
universally dominate. If either extreme dominates broadly: the mechanic is not creating a
decision. It is creating a tax or a trap. FAIL the candidate."

**§16 — Recovery gate.** [Measure durable recovery separately from profitability, per entry
class; improve player-findable recovery from non-terminal distress; do not make insolvency
magically reversible; recovery keeps time cost, cash/opportunity cost, residual failure risk;
"RECOVERY IS NOT FREE."]

**§17 — Runaway gate.** [Track P90/P95/P99, capital multiples, sustained high awareness, policy

immortality."]

**§18 — Success creates new pressure.** [Within the authorized system only; no invented costs

changing position; flops hurting; publicity consequence; release uncertainty. REMOVE: invisible

capital allocation; recovery; campaign strategy; risk; long-horizon identity.
