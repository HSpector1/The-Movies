## Purpose and boundary

This atlas defines an audio-editor vocabulary for the eight adjacent boundaries between the nine creative commissioning aliases. It helps the isolated Audio Lab test continuity without pretending that a commissioning alias is a runtime era, a date, or technology truth.

P13 owns global era and technology availability. A future typed P13 projection may make an audio alias eligible; this atlas may then describe presentation between eligible catalogues. It cannot advance time, unlock technology, infer adoption, or manufacture a historical transition. P05/P06 remain the future owners of authoritative Production and lot-activity inputs.

The palettes are commissioning guidance, not claims that every listed instrument or production method was universally present. Cultural sources must remain specific in future commissioning and human review; they are not interchangeable period flavor.

## Transition principles

1. Prefer common musical material, room tone, or studio-world ambience over a novelty time-machine effect.
2. Use authored natural endings when available.
3. Use phrase/bar boundaries only when metadata confidence is trustworthy.
4. Independently generated full mixes are horizontally switched variants, not aligned stems.
5. If compatibility is weak, allow silence and ambience to do the work.
6. Radio idents may acknowledge a format change in fiction but cannot announce unsupported history or mechanics.
7. Never reduce an era to static, vinyl crackle, bit crushing, modem sounds, a sweep, or a single “old radio” filter.
8. Preserve separate clean masters; period treatment is a presentation derivative.
9. Abruptness may be dramaturgically useful only when an upstream typed event requests it and the transition remains accessible.
10. Every planned or rendered treatment retains source identity, exact hashes, metadata confidence, and prototype-only status.

## Metadata contract

Each transition record must include:

- `transitionId`
- `outgoingCommissioningAlias`
- `incomingCommissioningAlias`
- `outgoingPrototypeId` and SHA-256
- `incomingPrototypeId` and SHA-256
- optional ambience/treatment IDs and SHA-256 values
- `constructionType`: `NATURAL_END_AMBIENCE`, `PHRASE_CROSSFADE`, or `BESPOKE_EXIT_ENTRY`
- exact start/end frames and sample rate
- DSP scheduling offsets
- fade duration and curve
- outgoing/incoming BPM and phrase estimates with confidence
- loudness and true-peak measurements
- mono compatibility result
- source and derivative lineage
- `historicalStatus`: `HISTORICAL_COMMISSIONING_ALIAS` or `EXTRAPOLATIVE`
- machine and human dispositions
- rights status
- fallback and refusal reasons

No renderer may silently time-stretch, pitch-shift, quantize, or resample one cue to force compatibility. Any deliberate offline sample-rate conversion is a derivative operation with exact provenance; it does not alter musical tempo.

## Runtime selection

An adjacent-era request is eligible only when a future upstream P13-owned payload supplies both outgoing and incoming eligible catalogue identities. The audio layer then:

1. validates both identities and hashes;
2. checks the transition atlas row and permitted treatment records;
3. filters by current speech ownership, music-density mode, accessibility mix, and file availability;
4. schedules the chosen treatment deterministically from the presentation seed;
5. emits a trace including boundary confidence, gains, fallback, and refusal;
6. leaves the authoritative upstream state untouched.

If there is no compatible or available treatment, the safe result is a natural exit to silence or eligible ambience followed later by an eligible incoming cue. A false-era substitution is forbidden.

## Radio and PA interaction

- Urgent PA/help owns speech over all radio material and suppresses idents.
- A typed functional bulletin outranks a decorative transition ident.
- A host link, advertisement, or ident cannot delay an expiring functional bulletin.
- A milestone sting is deferred while PA or functional voice owns the mix.
- A transition ident is decorative unless it is derived from a separately supplied typed functional payload.
- Captions are resolved before any functional voice begins.
- Radio disabled means no radio transition ident; music/ambience transition remains mechanically complete.

## Review questions

Every rendered transition needs blind Owner listening for:

- musical continuity and transition quality;
- era fit without cliché;
- loudness continuity and speech space;
- irritation after repetition;
- distinguishable but coherent palette change;
- mono, Night, Speech First, and Music Off behavior;
- whether silence is preferable;
- cultural specificity and historical credibility.

## Evidence boundary

This atlas supplies planned IDs, rules, and rejection criteria. It does not claim that the nine requested files were rendered, that DSP tests passed, or that anyone listened. Each completed artifact must be linked to an exact catalogue row, source hashes, render hash, and Audio Oracle scenario before its status can change from planned.
