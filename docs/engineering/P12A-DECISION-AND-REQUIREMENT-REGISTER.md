## Flag key

The flag column is ordered C / E / Q / D / O / F / X:

- C — P12A core.
- E — ready extension.
- Q — conditional, with the condition named.
- D — dependency-blocked, with the dependency named.
- O — Owner-blocked.
- F — deferred to a named future package/system.
- X — rejected feature or active prohibition.

“No” is written as “—”. A row can be a core seam and also dependency-blocked. “Current” is a recommended reconciliation for this pre-readiness packet, not an implementation order.

## Scope synthesis

- Player fantasy: a persistent fictional outside Hollywood that exists off-screen, spends conserved resources, employs persistent people, makes persistent films through causal projects, develops recognizable strategies, and leaves inspectable history. At the long-horizon success test the player can name the decades-long rival, the Star lost to it, the market-changing rival film, the studio that rose/declined/recovered/closed, and the competitor whose identity survived the campaign.
- P12A checkpoint: player identity plus three deterministic rival identities; persistent constrained business state; one-employer labor truth; exact rival projects and releases; separate-lane Industry views; typed receipts; honest migration; deterministic 6,240-week proof.
- Fidelity: Level 2 constrained project simulation. Rival physical traversal and low-level animation may be abstract; time, people, capacity, money, project identity, release identity, and consequences may not.
- Archetypes: seeded weighted policies, not rigid classes and not outcome guarantees. Public identity is inferred only from disclosed history.
- UX and visibility: the lot remains home; a retained Industry/Hollywood workspace provides charts, profile, film/person drill-down and factual Pulse. Exact public facts, event-gated facts, private facts, and not-yet-authorized facts remain distinct.
- Migration: add exact deterministic identity and only derivable player ownership; start rival/industry recording at the migration boundary; invent no earlier rival films, employers, rankings, market pressure, relationships, or founding facts.
- Proof: product-law, save/contract, Unity structure, deterministic machine, real-input, Owner-playtest, anti-façade, endurance, performance and cross-reference gates. Technical KEEP is not Owner acceptance.
- Endurance: primary campaign 1920–2040, approximately 6,240 weeks. Released films, people, career credits, meaningful studio events, and identity-reserving facts survive; scale is addressed through indexes, bounded views and deterministic summaries, not deletion.
- Exclusions: no random/timer films, fake market pressure, Power Ranking, live bidding/poaching, awards ceremony, technology race, churn, corporate systems, sabotage, or rival physical lots in P12A.

## Genuine unresolved Owner decision

### OWN-P12A-001 — migrated-rival historical ontology

- Decision: On a pre-P12 save, are the deterministically introduced rivals (A) already-existing current industry participants whose earlier detailed history is unavailable, or (B) genuinely new studios entering at the migration week?
- Why it matters: the choice changes historical truth, profile copy, entry events, founding/eligibility semantics, and what Legacy can later infer. It cannot be hidden as migration wording.
- Option A: persist founding as unknown, record registry/detail history from the migration boundary, and describe each as a known current participant with earlier detailed history Not Recorded.
- Option B: set entry/founding at the migration boundary and truthfully describe a new cohort entering then.
- Recommendation: Option A. It avoids the conspicuous fiction that several competitors all formed on upgrade day while inventing no films, employers, rankings, or founding date. Persist separate nullable founding, entry/registry, and recording-start fields so the distinction remains explicit.
- Default if deferred: Option A in planning only; implementation remains blocked from making the claim until approved.
- Dependents: P08 History/Awards eligibility, P12 profile/Pulse/migration, P15B entrants/churn, Hollywood Wire, Legacy.

No other new Owner decision is needed for P12A pre-readiness. In particular, immutable studioId, player naming, the minimum labor seam, fictional-Hollywood direction, 1920–2040 endurance, exclusion of competitive labor, three-rival proof size, no P12A market penalty, and no P12A Power Ranking are already settled. The P08 history-root arrangement, exact save roots/types, protocol, routes, coefficients, and measured budgets are engineering/reconciliation questions, not Owner inventions.
