- C — P12A core.
- E — ready extension.
- Q — conditional, with the condition named.

## Scope synthesis

- Player fantasy: a persistent fictional outside Hollywood that exists off-screen, spends conserved resources, employs persistent people, makes persistent films through causal projects, develops recognizable strategies, and leaves inspectable history. At the long-horizon success test the player can name the decades-long rival, the Star lost to it, the market-changing rival film, the studio that rose/declined/recovered/closed, and the competitor whose identity survived the campaign.
- P12A checkpoint: player identity plus nine persistent rival identities, proposed four opening and five staged entrants; persistent constrained business state; one-employer labor truth; exact rival projects and releases; separate-lane Industry views; typed receipts; honest migration; deterministic6,240-additional-advance proof including the eventual nine-rival workload.
- Fidelity: Level 2 constrained project simulation. Rival physical traversal and low-level animation may be abstract; time, people, capacity, money, project identity, release identity, and consequences may not.
- Archetypes: seeded weighted policies, not rigid classes and not outcome guarantees. Public identity is inferred only from disclosed history.
- UX and visibility: the lot remains home; a retained Industry/Hollywood workspace provides charts, profile, film/person drill-down and factual Pulse. Exact public facts, event-gated facts, private facts, and not-yet-authorized facts remain distinct.
- Migration: add exact deterministic identity and only derivable player ownership; start rival/industry recording at the migration boundary; invent no earlier rival films, employers, rankings, market pressure, relationships, or founding facts.
- Proof: product-law, save/contract, Unity structure, deterministic machine, real-input, Owner-playtest, anti-façade, endurance, performance and cross-reference gates. Technical KEEP is not Owner acceptance.
- Endurance: historical1920–2040 campaign vision retained; current proof is6,240 additional absolute-week advances, not an invented120-year calendar. Released films, people, career credits, meaningful studio events, and identity-reserving facts survive; scale is addressed through indexes, bounded views and deterministic summaries, not deletion.
- Exclusions: no random/timer films, fake market pressure, Power Ranking, live bidding/poaching, awards ceremony, technology race, churn, corporate systems, sabotage, or rival physical lots in P12A.
