# P07 implementation and Owner playtest lessons learned

**P07: OWNER ACCEPTED — KEEP — CLOSED.** The Owner accepted the actual combined
P06/P07 representative journey on 2026-09-04. The ruling was recorded at
2026-09-04T19:51:49Z; the exact playtest time was not supplied. No replay was
requested to turn the ruling into documentation.

Accepted identities, preservation evidence, and the precise downstream contract
are in the [final authority handoff](P07-TO-P08-FINAL-AUTHORITY-HANDOFF.md) and
[acceptance receipt](../campaigns/P07-OWNER-ACCEPTANCE-RECEIPT.md). These lessons
extend the earlier package lessons; they do not replace their history.

1. **Three result channels preserve useful disagreement.** Critics, Audience,
   and Business answer different player questions. Give each equal visual
   standing, canonical semantics, and its own evidence. The divided-response
   case demonstrates why praise can coexist with commercial loss.
2. **A universal Movie Quality score would erase the result.** Do not combine
   the channels or make one section appear to summarize the others. The Owner
   understood the accepted separate channels without such a score.
3. **Commitment is not release.** An exact-ID commitment records intent; it
   advances no time and creates no reception. The next authoritative week is
   the result-creation boundary. The Owner successfully played this distinction.
4. **An active run must not expose internal final truth as settled.** Locked
   full-run numbers remain public projections while active. Paid-to-date and
   projected totals need different labels; completion is the point at which
   final language becomes truthful.
5. **Gross, Studio Revenue, and Profit are different facts.** Keep gross,
   retained revenue, direct commitment, and contribution connected to their
   actual bases. Never relabel gross as profit, or imply direct film contribution
   includes unrecorded allocations of all studio costs.
6. **TypeScript owns result semantics; Unity renders them.** One verdict module
   supplies thresholds and bands, one derived view supplies result facts, and
   the generated contract carries them. Unity may format numbers and map an
   authoritative enum to words; it must not create a competing verdict engine.
7. **Same-title history requires immutable IDs and useful context.** Join and
   navigate by production/result ID. Show release context to help people tell
   records apart. Current `releaseWeek`/`weeksAgo` context is not an extra unique
   identity, and the wire does not contain a frozen historical title field.
8. **Released films need durable inspection after the active rail.** Keep result
   and run records when a theatrical run finishes. P07's FILM HISTORY group is
   inside the Film Result workspace; a separate always-visible history entrance
   is not yet shipped. Future history work must use that actual seam and carry
   the entrance limitation explicitly.
9. **Reveal availability need not seize control.** DETAILS opens the exact
   result without forced modal, camera motion, or time cost. A row-body click,
   selection, and an explicit open action should remain comprehensible through
   the normal input route. A released film needs no fabricated Locate target.
10. **Legacy result surfaces must not grow into competing authorities.** The
    grandfathered IMGUI memo remains critic-only and has a drift guard. Owner
    comprehension of the new route is now established, but removal remains
    future authorized work; acceptance closeout is not permission to edit it.
11. **Old saves cannot acquire fabricated history.** Preserve existing legacy
    settlement semantics. Do not backfill absent participants, forecasts,
    receipts, or historical Standing deltas. Future “Not Recorded” presentation
    must distinguish absent facts from a truthful zero or completed legacy run.
12. **The workspace should explain without becoming a spreadsheet.** Place
    release context and the three channels together, use clear financial tense,
    and keep operational rail rows free of gross/profit/ROI detail. The Owner
    accepted this division of attention.
13. **Presentation must consume no gameplay RNG.** Viewing, selecting, saving,
    reconnecting, and deriving a result again must preserve gameplay truth.
    Existing W6 evidence establishes pure projection and identical save/load
    continuation; documentation closeout does not require repeating that matrix.
14. **Failed HID runs belong in the evidence trail.** Classify timeout, seed,
    menu-ownership, and visible-hit-rectangle failures by cause. A harness-level
    PASS cannot substitute for completed journey steps. Runs 1–5 remain recorded;
    accepted journey G completed 15/15. Tightening BLOCKED/overall reporting is
    still a deferred harness non-blocker.
15. **Future narrative/audio consumers consume facts without creating them.**
    Wire may later interpret authoritative receipts; Radio remains downstream.
    Neither changes a P07 result, creates an award from result magnitude, or
    fills missing history. P08 Standing/history facts require their own authority.

The closeout also exposed two preservation lessons. First, resolve runtime,
tests/docs, build-manifest, candidate-packaging, and technical-seal SHAs
separately: the reported TS product checkpoint was actually a test/docs commit.
Second, verify packaged evidence against the evidence index: five canonical
Oracle directories are inside the preserved candidate and the sixth same-title
scenario remains at its original external evidence path. Record that fact and
its matching build evidence without modifying the accepted candidate.

Remaining polish is carried in the execution handoff with explicit dispositions:
small-viewport heading overlap and blocked-run summary semantics are deferred
non-blockers; memo retirement, broader visual direction, and future history
entrance ownership are future-package work. The accepted human journey remains
the final player-facing gate, beyond technical promotion and automated proof.
