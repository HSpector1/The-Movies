## 6. Persistence, isolation and performance findings for P14 state

**Persistence shape.** P14 state is one new top-level key per accepted wave, additive, exact-keyed, version-pinned inside itself the way `HollywoodState.version` is, validated by strip-and-delegate, seeded empty with a `recordingStartedWeek` on migration, guarded against downgrade in every older builder, and accompanied by a projection/schema bump with the then-outgoing schema id registered as a prior. P14A.1's only new persisted shapes are the market case, its proposals (each with the required `representation` key pinned to `null`) and the chooser receipt. The anti-exploit floor needs **no** new persisted fact: it is read from the accepted employment ledger through the per-person employment index (a player row ended early with no same-studio successor row starting at `endedWeek`), so it is NOT RECORDED for releases before `hollywood.originWeek`. The versioned termination rule needs no new fact either: it is discriminated by the root's own `recordingStartedWeek` through a P12 validator policy parameter. The Core checkpoint carries no P12 leaf widening: the rival's proposal terms are derived from its five existing policy fields, and the rival `termination` movement kind is Ready work.

**Isolation.** Guaranteed structurally, exactly as P12 guarantees it, on two accepted invariants: all P14 authoritative state lives inside `GameState` and every derived index is keyed by the identity of the immutable in-state object it derives from (the `GameState` at the bridge, the authoritative array or root in core); and no `GameState` object graph is ever shared by two sessions or records (every construction re-imports from JSON) and every reducer is copy-on-write. Receipts are stamped with in-state facts only; idempotency is scoped to the in-state lineage; no session, storage or campaign UUID enters core (`seed` is world identity, shared by every Save As copy). Worldgen and authored `PersonId`s are identical across every campaign regardless of seed, and up to 32 hydrated campaigns coexist in one process at library load, which is why nothing P14 stores may be keyed by `PersonId`, seed or `worldId`. Save As copies of one campaign carry the same open case, the same promises and the same relationships at the moment of copying and diverge afterwards, which is the correct and only lawful behavior. The acceptance test is not a byte comparison of the inactive record (the accepted precedent) but the in-process shape of skeleton item 5: Save As a campaign with an open case, settle the copy, Load the original and assert no outcome leaked, in both directions, plus a static gate that P14 adds no module-level container keyed by a value.

**Migration honesty.** Old saves initialize empty P14 roots and a recording boundary; nothing before it is reconstructed: no prior offers, competitors, promises, trust, relationships, romances, birthdays, retirement decisions or profession changes. `legacy_age_anchor` records the stored age (float or integer) at the migration week with estimated-year precision. The validator enforces the boundary the way the accepted validators do ('fabricated migration past', 'entry recording boundary mismatch'): every P14 row is dated ≥ `recordingStartedWeek`; every anchor's `migrationWeek` equals it and its `ageAtMigration` equals the preserved leaf; grandfathered eligibility due weeks are ≥ the boundary. The one labeled exception the package puts to the Owner is a truthful *reconstruction* of collaboration counts and of trust drivers older than the boundary (companion §7.3 Q3), for which the accepted `existing-player-contract` observation at migration is the precedent.

**Performance.** P12's disclosed qualifications are summarized here and are not resolved by P14; the [receipt](https://github.com/HSpector1/The-Movies/blob/13370d428f0693f3279732f6f4cc360a7fcaa4df/docs/campaigns/P12-R05-OWNER-ACCEPTANCE-RECEIPT.md) and the [attestation](https://github.com/HSpector1/The-Movies/blob/13370d428f0693f3279732f6f4cc360a7fcaa4df/evidence/p12a/final-native-20260911/performance-attestation.md) remain the authority. Large-world complete Save p95 **9.58 s** (9,577.75 ms, 20 samples after 3 warmups); native Save response **10.54 s**, first observed re-enabled about 11.79 s, and completion latency is not a measured freeze duration; **UI-unresponsiveness duration not measured** (separate frame samples do not establish Save-time input responsiveness); load-ready p95 13.415 s; 4.821 GB standalone high-water memory; serialize-plus-digest p95 199.5 ms against a 100 ms target; Hollywood field 37,829,874 bytes against 8 MB; average film 3,146 bytes against 1.5 KB; the 53.56 ms durable-write p95 covers only its already-encoded component; uncached Industry index construction ~403–405 ms (n = 1); the 12-active / 48-project / 4,000-film / 50-archived-studio stress and the isolated write-worker handoff unrun; intermittent Reconnecting observations recorded; permanent history and strict import remain binding constraints.

The binding size limits are three fail-closed ceilings fixed together in `bridge/runtime-checkpoint.ts` 157-164 and one in the library: `maxJournalBytes` **64 MiB** (the reference world's single Save receipt measured 49,595,194 B, ≈ 17 MB of headroom), `maxCheckpointBytes` **192 MiB** (measured 134,753,428 B), `maxJournalEntries` 512, and `CAMPAIGN_LIBRARY_MAX_BYTES` **256 MiB** (measured 70,593,125 B encoded). Each save byte is carried about three times in the decoded checkpoint and once as a journal entry, so the journal bound binds first. The mechanisms that make these costs scale with root size are known from the source: every accepted command, including each weekly advance, digests the current and saved saves, canonicalizes the journal, strictly re-imports and re-exports the current save, the saved slot and every journaled Save receipt, and canonicalizes the full outer checkpoint; a library write re-packs the working checkpoint and the active record (gzip level 1, twice over the same bytes) and re-canonicalizes the outer library over the cached packs of unchanged records; startup unpacks, strictly re-imports and re-exports every record. Consequences the package adopts: no per-week rows and no per-person-per-week evaluation output; due-week buckets as in-state structures so weekly work is O(due events) (PERF-002); relationship edges only after evidence, hot index bounded, drift computed on read, and history compacted under the rule of companion §5.5 registered against PERF-010 (uncompacted, the reference world's ~2,900 rival films × 15–21 seat pairs already exceed the August 25,000-driver envelope; the measured estimate at the reference world, films × pairs × bytes, is owed by the P14B wave before it seals); history stored in append order and paged as slices; settlement, retirement and cohort commits inside the weekly advance's single transition, never as separate commands; every P14 root measured in the accepted encoding against the journal, checkpoint and library bounds and their measured consumption before the wave seals; P14A.1 adds one case root and must not measurably move Save p95 or the weekly-advance latency on the protected library; the P14B/C roots are the ones that need the measurement.
