## 1. Mission

Create one technically sealed, reversible foundation chain that lets the player:

- understand current studio reputation and durable history;
- begin on a sparse 1920 property and physically build the minimum studio required to make a movie;
- understand and navigate the persistent people who make those movies;
- complete one full first-film journey into the accepted P07 result surface;
- save/load/reconnect without losing identities, context, or history;
- perform one final human acceptance pass after all three foundations are present.
