# P06A W5b — Living Studio Command Layer: completion & provenance

Honest per-item mapping of charter Appendix B (§19) to what satisfies it. LSCL is a
**bounded presentation refinement over EXISTING accepted authority** — one genuine P06 gap
(the rail's lifecycle reach) was built new; the other four surfaces were already delivered by
prior accepted waves and are re-verified here, not rebuilt. No new simulation mechanics; no
bridge/engine change; the engine bundle and the six oracle fixtures stayed frozen.

## 1. Movie progress rail — **NEW (this wave)**

`StudioMovieRailContracts` (pure, Infrastructure assembly, 8 EditMode tests) +
`StudioProductionRailHud` extension. The rail was Development-only; it now carries **every
active production**, keyed by exact productionId, across the full vocabulary
DEVELOPMENT / CASTING / PRODUCTION / POST / RELEASE READY / COMMITTED. Ready and committed are
distinct chips (the hold law made visible). Each row: lifecycle chip, title, the authority's
own time-or-waiting line, an action-required (decision) vs blocked (queue) glyph — word +
shape, never colour alone — and a Locate body that WITHHOLDS rather than no-ops (#16).
Productions take the capped slots first; screenplay rows fill the rest, so the first-film
journey (zero productions) is byte-for-byte the pre-LSCL rail and the P04A owner-input
geometry is untouched. Every existing rail law preserved (peripheral, yields to
card/inspection/workspace, ghost shield, LOCATE CASTING secondary action). Full floor 730/730.

## 2. Top HUD — **already delivered (StudioLivingTimeHud)**

Renders WHEN (era masthead · authoritative week — never a derived date), HOW FAST (Pause /
1× / 2× / 4×), cash + weekly direction (arrow + sign, never colour alone — no ungoverned
forecast), and a truthful attention/pause-reason line sourced from the authority's own
journey `next.label` / `blocked.reason`. Under the P06A hold law the living clock legitimately
keeps rolling past a held Release-Ready picture (nothing auto-releases; committing is the
player's optional act), so the release *decision* is surfaced persistently and
non-blockingly in the rail chip (RELEASE READY ▸ DECIDE) and the Post building cue rather than
as a forced HUD stop. Truthful, not a regression.

## 3. Building attention — **already delivered (per-owner attention)**

Attention belongs to the place that owns the work: the Development building's pennant, the
Casting Office's own management-distance attention state (`CastingAttentionContracts`, only
when a project awaits the player), and the W4 Post building world cues
(idle / waiting / active-finishing / release-ready / committed). Restrained, place-local, no
repeated global alerts.

## 4. Talent access — **already delivered (Casting Office workspace) + rail assignments**

The Casting Office is a persistent, discoverable world location (`StudioWorkspaceHost` /
`CastingBuildingStableId`) whose retained workspace exposes hiring candidates, freelancers,
the "N of M slots busy" capacity line, and the P05A.3 casting-shortage → Find an Actor remedy
route — from existing market/roster facts, a candidate never shown available-now, no
contract-authority bypass. Per-production assignments (who/where) now also read at a glance on
the movie rail. No broad HR game.

## 5. Lot life — **already delivered (LL-CP7)**

`StudioDecorativeIdentity` is the single decorative-identity law; `StudioLotLifePresentation`
`Strip`s it onto every spawned extra so decorative bodies carry no authoritative person id,
no payroll/progress/blocker/outcome meaning, may be reduced to zero without changing
simulation, and never masquerade as named talent.

## Anti-patterns (research delta) — held

No spreadsheet rail (compact portfolio rows only); no colour-only state (word + glyph carry
every chip); no workspace that erases the lot (the rail yields to deeper surfaces; retained
workspaces keep the visible lot edge). The rail never becomes gameplay authority (#15): it
selects/Locates only, commits nothing, and is never required before clicking the physical
world owner.
