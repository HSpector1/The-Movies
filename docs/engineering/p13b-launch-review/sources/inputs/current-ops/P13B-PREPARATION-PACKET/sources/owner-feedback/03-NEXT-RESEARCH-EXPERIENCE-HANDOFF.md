## Usability obligations to take into the next playable slice

### Laboratory entry

Selecting the Laboratory should expose one unmistakable primary action to manage research. Use a clear action label, location and visual hierarchy within the existing lot/detail owner. Avoid adding another competing workspace or requiring the Owner to know hidden navigation.

The specific final label is an implementation recommendation to validate, not an Owner-authored string.

### Funding, actual spending and staffing

Show approved ceiling, usable/actual R&D spend, active staffing, the binding limit, progress and completion estimate together.

For the current one-Scientist example:
- Approved ceiling: $40,000/week.
- Usable research spend: $10,000/week at saturation.
- The additional $30,000 of ceiling is not an expenditure.
- Salary and employment overhead are separate continuing costs and must come from their existing authoritative producers.

Do not tell the player to hire another Scientist as an immediately available remedy in a build that does not allow it. Distinguish a build limitation, an authored staffing cap, a missing employee and insufficient funding.

### Completion versus installation

Keep knowledge/research completion, access, physical work and operational capability distinct.

At completion, show the exact affected building, current capability, quoted work, cost, downtime, completion date and next legal action. Do not imply that a purchase on Stage 7 necessarily means installation already completed or every stage acquired sound.

### Idle staff

When Otto has no active research, state why and what costs continue. Distinguish no implemented project, a future research window, a missing prerequisite, a paused task and a staffing/equipment blocker.

Do not auto-fire him, invent productive work, or pretend an unimplemented project becomes available on a known date. Where lawful contract controls exist, link to their existing owner rather than inventing new release or severance rules.

## Depth obligations

The P13A companion already preserves staffing to full capacity, cooperation/splitting, persistent queues and a second useful research brief with a real consumer.

For P13B preparation, recommend which existing catalogue briefs make those capabilities meaningful. At least two useful choices must be comparable at the intended review point; a second empty progress bar does not establish depth. Also describe what the player can do after finishing the first project, or explain any genuine historically gated gap.

A brief must change an actual task, facility capability, cost or production decision. Do not add a generic quality bonus merely to keep Otto occupied.

Do not guarantee permanent researcher utilization: an economically meaningful idle period is allowed, but it must be explained and manageable. More researchers should create a visible time/cost tradeoff; more laboratories should create a genuine cooperation-versus-parallelism decision.
