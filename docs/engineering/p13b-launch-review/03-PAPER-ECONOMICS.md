## Formula and clock

At start week w, an active eligible project advances over [w,w+1), pays that full week’s usable R&D, and completes at w+1 if enough work accrued. Last-week output is capped, the weekly charge is not prorated, matching delivered R01. A physical job committed at c for d weeks completes at c+d; its first operating debit occurs on advance [c+d,c+d+1). Thus through horizon H the charge count is max(0,H−c−d). Salaries and overhead use [employment start,end exclusive), whether busy or idle.

For eligible participating seats n, spend=min(project ceiling,10000n); raw output=n+spend/20000. One Lab factor=1; two equal full Labs factor=13/16, so output=12×13/16=9.75, total speedup=1.625 against six units from one full Lab. Weeks=ceil(remaining work/output). General unequal-team rule is in companion §4. Signing=18720n, salary=2000×person-weeks, employment overhead=1500×person-weeks. Base studio overhead=15000×horizon weeks is shown separately. No revenue, return on investment or interest is invented.
