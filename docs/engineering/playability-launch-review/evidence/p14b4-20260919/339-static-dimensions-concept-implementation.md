## 1. Exact concept-map owner price

The calculator still visits EVERY original concept, paying the existing16
scalar steps, both actual ID/title spans, ID-character accumulation, title-width
maximum and original occurrence count. It no longer executes the nested growing-
prefix Map-bill expression tree on each row.

The replacement is the exact adopted identity:

```text
K(i,Si,Li) = 1 + Li + i*(1+Li) + Si
sum(10+K) = 10N + N(N+1)/2 + N*sum(Li)
```

Each Li appears i+1 times directly and N-1-i times in subsequent stored-prefix
terms: N times total. N counts occurrences, not distinct keys, preserving even
the previous conservative duplicate overcount. Actual concept Map/tuple owner
construction, writer lookups/labels and market invocation remain unchanged.

Parity/control pays16 before evaluation. The EVEN factor is halved before the
paid saturating product: `(N/2)*(N+1)` or `N*((N+1)/2)`. N=0 remains zero, N=1
remains one. N is the count of a genuine native concept array; N+1 is a safe
integer. Each argument expression has its prepaid calc16 receiver; the final
two products and three-term sum have prepaid receivers and the existing paid
saturating helpers. There is no saturated product followed by division.

All operands are nonnegative, so capped algebra preserves the previous owner
reservation. Savings are solely removed calculator expressions, not reduced
owner charges, refunds or presumed cache warmth.
