## Material corrections before design acceptance

1. **Bind valuation to its date and prevent future information.** `libraryValuationV1(state, holderId, asOfWeek)` currently describes *all* released works and a fold over title events without saying `releaseWeek <= asOfWeek`, `createdWeek <= asOfWeek`, or `event.week <= asOfWeek`. The R1 option premium's `maxQuality` can therefore see a future release; the R1/R2 holder can be a later buyer. Define a valid query range and as-of fold, including same-week event ordering and archived/nontradeable treatment. Include a RED where a later film and a later title sale are both present in state but are invisible to an earlier query. Do not apply the eventual R2 owner to an earlier film-value date. This is required for a deterministic, causally truthful read model, beyond coefficient tuning.
2. **Resolve empty-root migration versus complete legacy valuation.** The proposed predecessor migration writes an empty P16 root and materializes historical titles only on first lawful P16 use. The valuation sums only films with an R2 title. Thus immediately after migrating a valid Save45 with released films, a read-only valuation can return zero or omit those films, and its result changes merely because another use materialized title. Specify either a pure virtual historical-title resolver for read queries, or deterministic materialization at a named lawful boundary before publication. Read-only valuation must not mutate state. Test equal valuation before and after materialization and save/reload, subject to known terminal/estate facts. Ambiguity must cause an explicit unavailable/refusal result, not a silent zero or caller windfall.
3. **Represent genuinely historical dates and provenance.** Authored-start films have `released: {kind:'beforeCampaign', year}` and canonical `filmId`, `conceptId`, `title`, `studioId`, but no `FilmConcept` row or campaign `releaseTick`. A `createdWeek`/`releasedWeek` integer and concept-derived `titleAtCreation` cannot truthfully encode that source. Use a tagged historical/campaign date and identify the authentic title and creator source for each of authored-start, pre-P16 player, and pre-P16 simulated rival paths. Do not invent a week-zero greenlight, a concept record, or a founding transaction for authored films. Historical title events need an explicit genesis kind that retains the source date/manifest and orders before campaign events. Include authored-origin tests that join to the starting manifest and survive save/reload.
4. **Define title-event chain validity, including founders.** The contract says the first founding event has creator as holder, then later says closed sources may require estate/archive disposition and materialization can be refused. State that a legacy genesis event records *historical creator ownership at creation*, while current saleability is determined from genuine subsequent events or typed terminal handoff. Validate subject/right compatibility, exact one genesis, unique stable event IDs and ordinals, strictly canonical chronological order, `priorHolder` continuity, current lawful holder, and prohibition on future or duplicate events. A closed source with no authoritative terminal disposition cannot be treated as an active saleable creator title. Make the RED isolate those chains from otherwise valid predecessor states.

## Source-grounded observations and retained rules

- The proposed `story-property:` plus complete concept ID is injective for actual `c-NN`, `concept-orig-NNNN`, `${studioId}:concept:${ordinal}` (with `uniqueIdentity` suffix), and `${studioId}:historical-concept:${index}` forms if P16 validates one distinct property per founding concept and reserves its own namespace. The draft's conditional wording is sensible; finish collision, length, and inverse validation before RED. Do not silently truncate or normalize the source ID.
- Generated player/rival reception clamps critic score to 0–100 (`reception.ts`), authored scores are validated to 0–100 (`hollywoodValidation.ts`), and `campaignLegacy.ts` requires 0–100. The frozen Save8 film leaf in `save.ts` checks finite numbers only. P16 must explicitly validate 0–100 on every joined old film, including genuine older saves, before computing. Refuse malformed input, do not clamp it into value. The proposed `$100,000` film and `$25,000` R1 option coefficients are clearly provisional charter choices, not Owner prices; integer rounding and per-ID once-only attribution are appropriate after the temporal and legacy fixes. Also specify rounding and numeric safety for fractional scores and many films.
- The R1 property, R2 released FilmId, R3 exclusive continuation term, and R4 scoped property/film cross-media term separation matches the reviewed integration law. Keep creator immutable, live holder event-derived, licences as surviving encumbrances, no generic R2 licence, no executable R4 before a P18 consumer, and no independent branch market before explicit parent-sourced promotion. A later P16B active-run R2 sale needs its separate receipt-assignment producer; P16A should keep it refused. The draft preserves these boundaries.
- `FilmIdentity.studioId` is creator evidence, not a current holder. For player films, use `FilmResult.productionId`/`conceptId` and `releasedFilms` with authentic player identity; for simulated rival films, use `filmId`/`result.productionId`/`conceptId` plus Hollywood release receipt; for authored films use the starting manifest. A title must join each source exactly once. A shelved concept may have development facts but no R2 film. Predecessor save fixtures must first pass their own frozen reader; a new-era refusal then isolates one P16 rule, without repaired or invented historical facts.
- RED items for live grant overlap, transfers, branch promotion and P18 authority are valuable interface tests only where a real producer exists; label future P16B/P17/P18 integration tests separately from P16A's empty licence root and pure lookup/schema tests. Do not claim those tests executed at P16A.
