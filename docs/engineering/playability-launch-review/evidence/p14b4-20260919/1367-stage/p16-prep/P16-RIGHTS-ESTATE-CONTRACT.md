## 3. Four rights, exact subjects and title

Adopt the corrected P16 four-right model for the first slices:

| Right | Subject and holder | Permitted instrument |
|---|---|---|
| R1 property | One StoryProperty and its inherent continuation authority; exactly one current holder | Whole-title sale. R1 itself is not a term licence; granted R3/R4 interests remain encumbrances. |
| R2 film/library | One identified released FilmId; exactly one current holder, independent of R1 and immutable creator | Whole-title sale. No generic R2 licence; cross-media uses R4. |
| R3 continuation | Sequel/remake/spin-off authority on a specified parent property; default authority derives from its R1 holder | Exclusive term licence only, not an independently sold title. Scope explicitly enumerates the permitted continuation use. |
| R4 cross-media | Explicit property or film subject, derived from its relevant R1/R2 holder | Exclusive or nonexclusive term licence; no executable grant before the P18 consumer exists. |

P16 is the sole title/licence writer. P17/P18 request and consume its facts. Use one Licence instrument with subject/right/scope, current lawful grantor, grantee, exclusivity, start/end-exclusive weeks, consideration, expiry/reversion terms and transaction provenance. No territorial subdivisions in the initial slice. Refuse overlapping exclusive scopes, unknown subjects, expired permission, grants wider than the grantor holds and sublicensing absent explicit authority. A title sale preserves live licences; it cannot reset their term or erase the grantee.

Adopt original greenlight as the new-property mint boundary: property ID and first title event appear atomically with the original production. Store original concept, creator studio, creation week/title and append-only work links. A licensed continuation links to the existing parent and does not mint an unencumbered R1. Under 1367-O a spin-off character or branch stays under that same parent until a separately recorded, explicitly authorized promotion; promotion must name its source parent and preserve existing grants rather than laundering them into new rights.

For pre-P16 works, lazily materialize identity/title from genuine existing creation and transaction facts at first required transaction/continuation. No global campaign rewrite, invented historical sales or new ownership awarded to the caller. Historic creator is the initial holder only absent a later genuine title event; closed sources route to the actual estate/archive state. Keep creator identity distinct from current holder. Archived ownership remains traceable but nontradeable; never represent it as an unowned free asset.

An R1 sale or exclusive R3 grant touching an active production requires a lawful work transfer or an explicit retained production licence. Otherwise refuse. For R2 sales during an active run, this contract selects explicit remaining-receipt assignment at the close boundary: receipts settled before the boundary remain with the prior holder; later receipts follow the named assignee. No simultaneous price netting of those same future receipts. The run producer must support this before that sale is enabled; otherwise refuse the active-run transaction. Never alter original release credit or re-award Standing.

Library valuation is derived display/transaction input only, with no periodic cash, Ranking/Standing award or instant reissue. Co-held R1/R2 cannot both count the same released catalogue value. R1's separate option premium must be separately sourced; unproduced/shelved projects gain no automatic catalogue valuation.

## 4. Closure due, actual closure and the estate handoff

1357-A §7 is exact: `closed` condition/closure-due settles nothing. Until the Wave 4 actual transition, the studio still pays installments, trades, advances work and runs, and appears under the existing operating rules. No P16 estate, rights grab, free agency or terminal filtering may be triggered merely by that condition value.

Wave 4 builds and validates one atomic actual closure result. At its defined phase boundary it records the cause/week, changes the P12 operating state once, closes each live employment interval once, removes each person from operating assignments into lawful free agency in that closure week, and emits one typed disposition for every open subject. The closure week is the first nonoperating boundary; work/money already settled earlier in that tick stays settled and is not replayed. The producer charter must pin the exact existing phase insertion and loan stop convention with a boundary trace, retaining 1357-F's global P15 sequence/phase discipline. Do not invent a new independent clock.

The closure record must distinguish (a) operations have ended, (b) each disposition has been applied or remains explicitly pending, and (c) each claim's actual amount paid/unpaid. Atomic publication of a complete account is not a claim that all debts have been paid. Publish actual closure promptly; rival public views retain only stage/band before closure, without its private countdown. Player closure ends the playable run and loads read-only closure/history mode. Wave 5 warning/distress/closure notices and player command parity are required before enabling playable player settlement.

The handoff includes stable closure/estate/studio IDs, terminal week, finite opening/closing weeks, ordered typed lot IDs, subject/title/encumbrance references, disposition status, cash reconciliation and claim references. It contains no employment ownership. P16 can transact only lots explicitly admitted by this P15 handoff; it cannot declare a failing studio terminal. The lot window is half-open `[opensWeek, closesWeek)` and closes once. Unsold lots archive deterministically at the terminal window boundary; no indefinite refresh or new right free claim. Player end-of-run display need not advance rivals to finish that window: actual pending or archived status remains explicit.

Required per-subject dispositions include employment, development/screenplays, committed productions, theatrical runs/remaining receipts, facility plans and installed capacity, research/access history, cash and loan remainder, property/film titles and existing licences. Every subject gets one disposition; every mutation has its receipt. A count/digest is an integrity aid, not a substitute for resolvable typed records. Retain historical businesses or an equivalent validated terminal history record so old costs, labs, seats, contracts and films still resolve. Do not delete labs merely to empty an operational array.

Claims retain source obligation ID, claimant identity/kind, original amount, actual paid amount, unpaid amount, payment references and final recorded status. Enforce `original = paid + unpaid` unless a separately authorized adjustment with its own reason/source exists. No fabricated creditor for negative cash, no paid claim without an actual debit, no payment above the claim, no claim erased by later employment. P15 owns claim class/priority/payment policy; P16 sends sale proceeds to estate accounting only and cannot settle claims itself. Existing loan-v1 unpaid remainder is recorded at closure and is not silently paid by a buyer or a new lender. The Wave 4 charter must adopt the exact employment-claim basis and treatment of existing negative cash before coding; R02's illustrative full-guarantee and overdraft claims are not current executable law.

Every estate worker is freely recruitable under ordinary P14 law after closure. An estate buyer pays no retention priority fee, obtains no reservation and cannot extinguish that worker's closure claim by hiring them. Frozen production credit/participant history remains factual, not a right to compel new work. A transferred unfinished production can resume only after the work producer proves lawful present commitments for every required participant and capacity; otherwise it stays suspended/disposed according to its declared lot terms. No silent cast substitution or free completion.

## 5. Healthy full absorption and retention

The initial healthy target must be willing and stable-stage both at quote and at closing. It may reject the offer. No distressed going-concern acquisition; ordinary single-asset sales use their own predicates. Full absorption leaves exactly the buyer's operating studio, with target identity/creator history permanently preserved and a dated end-of-independent-operations event. No autonomous subsidiary, second playable lot, transferred Standing or retained operating label. Authored future entrants remain scheduled; do not create replacements or an artificial minimum rival count.

Adopt corrected register C-7's initial-slice scope: the operating player studio is `NotForSale`; generate no inbound whole-studio offers and no player-initiated sale ending. Those are optional later features, not enabled by the generic willing-target predicate. After actual player closure, its estate follows the common disposal/accounting law, subject to the explicit no-extra-post-game-simulation rule.

The player explicitly chooses retention with a disclosed no-action default. Iterate the target's authoritative contract order, keeping only lawful contracts fitting usable capacity and the complete transaction's affordability. Preserve person-choice/relationship refusal rules, remaining term, salary and existing commitments. A continuation ends the target interval and mints a linked buyer-employer contract ID with identical remaining bargain, no duplicate signing payment and preserved original signing provenance. A fresh offer is ordinary P14 law and may be declined; it is not disguised continuation. The exact P12 reason values and linked validation must land before the first executable absorption.

Non-retained healthy staff get the lawful capped exit once, charged to the target in its closing account. Retained work cannot outlive its lawful required participants: continue the required participants and capacity through its obligations, or take an explicit producer-supported cancellation/write-off. The default cannot silently keep a film while dropping its busy cast, nor cancel work via an invented ordinary termination path. Selection preview quotes these coupled outcomes before acceptance; a stale preview is revalidated/refused atomically.

Adopt R02's healthy money separation: consideration is the agreed non-cash asset/business price; target cash transfers at par after its actual closing exits, including a negative amount. Closing outlay is consideration minus that post-exit cash, plus separately named real charges exactly once. Guarantees are disclosed beside value, never subtracted from consideration or book value. There is no `max(0, price - guarantees)` shortcut and no new credit for assuming normal payroll. Book, liquidation estimate, unfloored operating-value estimate and transaction price stay distinct. A healthy target carrying an outstanding v1 distress loan is refused by this initial contract because neither lawful early payoff nor assignment exists; this is a bounded implementation guard, not a permanent product ban on future ordinary loans.

Knowledge transfer uses new buyer provenance records citing completed verified source research and ordinary adoption prerequisites. No installation teleport or inventor price advantage. Cancel target in-flight research while retaining verified work/expenditure, historical seats/labs and original project IDs. Partial progress remains with its original studio/technology; it cannot be copied to a buyer's new project or refunded. No new P13 research redesign is authorized here.

Facilities do not move to the buyer. Any terminal liquidation credit must cite the actual source asset, admitted refund law, once-only disposition and accounting owner; no paper appraisal credited as cash, no blanket instrument refund and no reuse of recovery demolition permissions to delete terminal history. Facility terminal disposition is a Wave 4 producer seam and is not satisfied merely by recovery's lab-disposal helper.

## 6. Cash, disclosure, transaction protocol and archive

Every buyer must pass permitted-bidder checks before disclosure, offer/bid submission and closing. This contract adopts stable-stage-only bidders for the first slice: an entered, operating studio, with founding completed, condition exactly `stable`, and no outstanding P15B distress loan. Warning, distress, recovery, closure-due and actually terminal studios are ineligible. This is an explicit first-slice contract choice implementing the corrected register C-5 recommendation, not a claim that the Owner supplied this exact stage predicate. The loan guard applies independently even if the stage is stable. Disclosure additionally requires the real fee to be affordable while preserving the current-obligation reserve; a binding offer/bid or close requires the complete resulting-obligation test below.

No rescue-loan proceeds, new acquisition credit, implicit negative-cash financing or estate cash can fund the price. Cash after all actual closing charges must cover the versioned post-purchase reserve calculated from the buyer's resulting obligations, including retained contracts/work. Recheck at closing; an earlier quote is not a cash reservation. The versioned policy must bind concurrent accepted obligations so two outstanding bids cannot each spend the same cash. This contract chooses a commitment reservation against spendable cash while a binding bid/offer is outstanding, released on rejection/expiry and consumed once on closing.

Provisional numeric policy is a producer deliverable before transaction REDs: reserve duration, diligence fee, window duration, minimum/maximum quote age, bid duration, willingness premium, cooldown and holdback/resale protection. Canonical ~26-week reserve and 52-week holdback are candidate tuning only. Specify finite bounds and integer rounding in one versioned policy; do not infer them from illustrative research examples. No ordinary-sale second-bidder primitive. If the initial anti-flip policy requires a competing bid to waive a resale floor, bilateral sales cannot manufacture that exception; only a qualifying real estate competition can.

Due diligence is a bounded, costed, dated transaction event available only to a permitted bidder for a specified target/lot. Charge its real fee once and record recipient, target, disclosure week/version, exact scope, receipt ID and stale/revalidation policy. Reveal only relevant assets, liabilities/claims, rights/encumbrances, contract terms and quoted work/exit costs needed to price that deal. General rival views remain private and disclose no unrelated studio's finance. Rival policies receive the same lawful disclosure event, not a privileged private-object read. Pagination or bounded subject references must replace repeated full campaign snapshots; public receipts do not expose the disclosed private body to all studios.

Ordinary sales are bilateral quote → acceptance → atomic validated closing or refusal/expiry. No auction or hidden second bidder. Healthy full absorption uses this path and its retention preview. Preserve the seller's consent and pricing basis; due-diligence payment does not force a sale.

Estates alone use competitive bids on clean, explicitly identified lots. This contract adopts sealed one-shot comparison at the recorded window close, with descending valid bid amount then ascending reserved studio identity order as a deterministic tie-break. Disclose that rule in the bid contract; draw no RNG. Validate each buyer's eligibility/reserved cash and lot authority again before resolution; try the next eligible valid bid when a higher one no longer qualifies. No reopening the window, raising bids after observing rivals or selling the same right through overlapping lots. Lot overlap is refused when the handoff is constructed. The transaction records all applied debits/credits, selected bidder, rejected/expired bid disposition and exact title/licence effects.

Estate consideration goes to the estate once. It buys only the listed assets/rights and does not transfer the failed studio's cash, debt, people or operational label. Sales may provide actual proceeds for P15's lawful settlement, but unsatisfied claims do not become the buyer's debt. Archival freezes unsold rights as not for sale while preserving sold title chains, continuing licences, unpaid claims and creator/work history. A repeat close/archive command is an explicit refusal or internal policy no-op with no repeated money/title change.
