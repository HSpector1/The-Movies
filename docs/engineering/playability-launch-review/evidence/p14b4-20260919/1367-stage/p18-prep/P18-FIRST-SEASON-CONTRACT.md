One unit is credited only when the required valid people, occupied capacity and the paid weekly production cost all exist for that week. The advance is cash received with an unearned obligation, not earned operating revenue. Let E be cumulative earned fees, P gross commissioning cash actually received (including A), and R advance principal actually returned. Milestone payment is `max(0, E_new − P)` while live; ordinarily scripts consumes the advance ($0 cash), picture lock pays $240,000 and on-time delivery pays $240,000. No milestone is earned after cancellation. Revenue read models use earned milestone receipts, not advance cash. Every payment references agreement/milestone IDs; receiving or loading it twice cannot pay twice.

Add these precise sign and reconciliation rules to both finance owners under the new era; older eras continue refusing every TV kind.

## 7. Cancellation, rights loss, corporate failure and conservation

Before accepted delivery, the producer may cancel explicitly. Automatic cancellation occurs at final deadline failure, loss of required lawful grant authority, unrecoverable required-person loss, or actual P15B closure. Ordinary temporary capacity/cash shortage stalls until the deadline; it does not erase work. There is no random commissioner cancellation or new cancellation-market policy.

All such pre-delivery cancellations use the disclosed same v1 settlement: retain completed work/credits/costs and previously earned compensation; cancel unearned future milestones; return the unearned advance U; assess the separate $120,000 cancellation charge K once; release current season capacity and personal engagements once. The charge is a contractual production failure cost, not loan interest or an acquisition-finance product. It prevents paid partial milestones from being a free repeated-cancellation strategy and remains a provisional tuning hypothesis to measure. It never reduces historical earned fee receipts or retroactively rewrites them. There is no general automatic expense refund. No production work continues solely because money is still owed.

For an operating studio's voluntary/deadline/rights/person cancellation, freeze U0 and pay its outstanding amount from available nonnegative cash first, then K from the remainder; record any unpaid amounts as explicit agreement obligations due immediately. Do not create negative cash as a new borrowing route, claim an unpaid amount settled or erase it on reload. The public `settleTvObligations { agreementId, expectedRemaining, amount }` action requires expectedRemaining to equal the current total outstanding balance and accepts a positive integer amount no greater than both actual available nonnegative cash and that balance. Apply it to the remaining advance refund first, then the remaining cancellation charge, with exact receipts and updated R/paid totals. A stale repeated settlement request or duplicate payment receipt refuses without mutation; a fresh request may pay another lawful portion. No interest, refinancing, forgiveness or new funds arise from this action. Before making a new TV proposal, a rival uses the same action to pay available cash toward its oldest outstanding TV agreement, ordered by cancellation week then agreement ID; player and rival remain ineligible for a new season until all such arrears clear. Player repayment is explicit, not an invisible periodic debit.

These are existing-contract settlement claims, not a new finance product. On actual studio closure, publish the complete frozen refund/charge obligations and any payments already made to P15's common settlement owner; its claim priority and actual available estate cash determine further payment. The operating-studio action is then unavailable. P18 must not jump the estate creditor queue by debiting these obligations first inside the closure handler. Keep ledger/claim references and cumulative actual repayments linked so the same obligation cannot be paid both by P18 and P15.

Money invariant after a cancellation settlement attempt: `P − R = E + advanceRefundUnpaid`, and `K = cancellationChargePaid + cancellationChargeUnpaid`. Therefore net commissioning cash retained after cancellation-charge payments is `E + advanceRefundUnpaid − cancellationChargePaid`. Production spend, payroll, studio costs and rights consideration are additional actual debits, not offsets that make this identity disappear. Earned fee remains E even if net cash is negative. Examples: cancellation before first milestone has E=0, P=$120,000, refund obligation $120,000 and K=$120,000; after picture lock E=P=$360,000, U=0 and K=$120,000. Partial settlement keeps unpaid claims visible. Repeating cancellation performs no new work/payment/charge/ID allocation; the public duplicate command refuses, internal terminal policy is a no-op.

A live rights sale does not invalidate a licence that P16 says survives encumbered transfer. A lawful expiry/revocation/scope failure cannot be bypassed by stored admission snapshots: check actual permission before each work unit and delivery, and before availability. Admission's full-term requirement prevents ordinary known expiry from stranding the initial plan. If authority genuinely ends before delivery, cancel as above and preserve incurred obligations; future TV use stops. If a valid delivered distribution grant later legally ends early, stop availability on that boundary; earned commissioning fee and prior rights consideration remain recorded, with no invented clawback beyond actual P16 terms. An invalidated/forged grant fails validation rather than creating a compensating payment.

P15B actual closure cancels active production and releases staff in that week under the shared closure law. ClosureDue alone does not. A later estate asset purchase buys no workers or production permission; it cannot silently resume the cancelled agreement, erase earned compensation or clear U/K. Healthy absorption may carry a season only if P16's real grant/commissioner terms permit assignment and P12/P14 capacity/people and all settlement obligations transfer lawfully; otherwise cancel it in the seller's closing account. This first agreement chooses **nonassignable commissioning obligations**: any absorption of the producing studio before delivery cancels the agreement under the same disclosed terms. Delivered work/title can transfer encumbered through P16 while its existing distribution window continues. No added work-transfer implementation is hidden in this first route.
