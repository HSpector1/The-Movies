## 2. Exact boundary and phase proposal

Adopt new provisional `closure/v1` law. Define actual closure week C as the first nonoperating boundary. During the advance from C − 1 to C, ordinary work, receipts, payroll, facility costs and loan installments due at input week C − 1 settle normally. Final lifecycle/market settlement and Ranking then run. Wave 2 evaluates final week C facts and can declare closure due. Actual closure runs immediately afterwards, before Legacy freeze. Work done earlier in that advance remains factual; closure neither reverses nor repeats it. At input week C and later, the terminal studio cannot operate, borrow, hire, greenlight, conduct research or incur operating payroll, overhead, facility costs or scheduled loan debits.

Propose phase-order version 2: market 1, ranking 2, condition 3, `p15b.closure` 4, finale 5. Keep table version 1 and every old row unchanged. Each new P15 closure/estate event carries the current phase triple and the one global P15 sequence, plus its own stable domain ID. Process due studios in ascending studio ID. A deterministic closure-phase dispatcher also services already-open estates; it cannot mint independent clocks or allocators. P16 transaction receipts retain their own ownership and link to the enclosing settlement boundary. Final exact dispatch integration must be refreshed when P16 production lands.

Closure due alone still leaves all operations and installments intact. A migrated Wave 2 due record closes prospectively at its first actual closure phase, never backdated to the earlier condition event. Its cause references that event. No automatic closure during deserialization. If required notices or recovery opportunities were unavailable in that predecessor, do not enable playable closure until the Wave 5 compatibility route is explicitly implemented and reviewed; do not manufacture past notices.

Wave 4 adds a loan stop boundary linked to the real closure record. An installment due at d is charged iff d < C; without actual closure, Wave 2's original schedule remains unchanged. In a produced state at T, paid installment count is `clamp(min(T, C) − contractedWeek − 1, 0, 52)`, omitting C from the minimum while operating. Thus the installment due at C − 1 has already paid on arrival at C; the one due at C never pays. Closure remainder is total minus those actual scheduled debits. No synthetic charge at closure, interest accrual after closure, arrears conversion, refund or buyer assumption. A cash balance below zero does not undo an installment already booked as paid.

## 3. Employment claims, payments and cash

New provisional terminal employment policy: every still-live employment interval ended by actual closure receives one claim based on `terminationCost(contract, C)` using its real remaining term and salary. Expiry already applied at C creates no second exit claim. Prior termination or settlement creates no duplicate claim. A zero-value release still has a typed disposition and contract link. Closure can release busy staff only through the terminal work dispositions below; it is not a bypass of ordinary voluntary termination eligibility. End intervals and release assignments atomically, with a new exact closure reason and receipt. People become ordinary free agents in week C, regardless of claim payment. Later recruitment neither transfers nor extinguishes their claims.

This capped closure entitlement is newly proposed policy grounded in the existing exit formula. Do not call a full future guarantee an already-accrued wage. Historical payroll and signing amounts already debited, even into negative cash, are paid movements; do not invent duplicate unpaid wages, signing debt, compensation for nonexistent work or a generic overdraft creditor.

Each claim has stable ID, closure/studio ID, source obligation and claimant references, law version, original amount, paid amount, unpaid amount, and actual payment references. Enforce `original = paid + unpaid`, integer nonnegative amounts, source uniqueness and no payment above the remainder. Explicit statuses distinguish zero/paid, unpaid, partially paid, and unpaid archived; they cannot contradict the amounts. A loan closure balance is a separate, non-distributable recorded obligation under current loan v1: its remaining amount stays unpaid, with no settlement-payoff primitive, lender entity or debt forgiveness. It must be visible beside distributable claims, not counted as settled. Adding estate loan repayment would require a separately reviewed amendment to the no-early-payoff contract; this proposal does not add it.

New provisional distribution rule: preserve the studio's existing authoritative cash/account, including a negative closing balance. Actual estate receipts credit that same account through explicit estate money kinds. Available distribution cash is `floor(max(0, cash − binding reservations))` after actual receipts, in the existing whole currency unit of the employment claim. The floor leaves any fractional theatrical-revenue residue in the real account; it does not round or rewrite past revenue. A receipt may reduce a negative balance without creating a creditor payment; show that reconciliation separately. Never reset negative cash to zero, issue new borrowing, debit a claim beyond available cash, or create a second estate balance with free opening funds.

At closure and after each estate receipt batch, pay employment claims in ascending original contract ID, then claim ID, taking `min(availableCash, unpaid)` for each. This deterministic priority is a new implementation default; it is not a claim about real insolvency law. It applies identically to player and rivals and permits partial payment. Source IDs, not talent fame, buyer preference or array position, determine order. Each positive payment has exactly one debit and a claim-linked payment receipt; zero payment has no money row. Existing source has no other enforceable terminal payable class. Before any future licence, cameo or other producer introduces a cancellation/refund obligation, extend this closed claim union and its explicit priority by reviewed versioned law; an unknown live obligation blocks that producer's integration rather than being silently discarded. Do not run terminal closure while a reachable obligation lacks a disposition.

Any positive residue with no payable claims remains recorded estate cash. No shareholder payout, liquidation revenue or balance forgiveness is inferred. Window archival preserves residual cash and all unpaid obligations honestly.
