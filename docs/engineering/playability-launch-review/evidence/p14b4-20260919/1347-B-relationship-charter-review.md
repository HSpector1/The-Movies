## Blocking defects

**1. Mentor completeness test does not address the same defect it uses to exclude genesis people (HIS-014, charter §2.4).**

The charter's test for "authoritative history establishes those first three" is `A is named in a CohortReceipt`. Its own justification for *excluding* genesis people is: "Genesis people get age-scaled genre experience and a zero 'this run' work counter (`worldgen.ts:513-514`, `:536`)... so their early careers are not recorded" — i.e., `genreExperience` is treated as evidence of an *implied, unrecorded* pre-campaign career.

But cohort entrants go through the **identical** generation path: `careerLifecycle.ts:377` calls `generateIndustryTalent(state.seed, id, role, undefined, exact)`, which calls `generateTalent`, which unconditionally runs the same `buildGenreExperience` step (`worldgen.ts:394-421`) that seeds genesis people. The only difference is the age multiplier: `expAgeMult = clamp(0.6 + (age - 20) / 60, 0.6, 1.4)` (`worldgen.ts:400`) — at the top of the cohort age range (29, `tuning.ts:433`: `lo: 20, hi: 29`), that's 0.75×, against a base draw of `truncatedNormal(GEN_EXP_MEAN=12, SD=12, LO=0, HI=60)` (`tuning.ts:262-265`). A 29-year-old cohort entrant can plausibly draw a non-trivial genre-experience value (up to ~45 on a 0–100 scale) through the exact same mechanism the charter cites as disqualifying for genesis people.

The charter's own §6 item 5 ("Mentor completeness... confirm no unmanaged production can seat a cohort entrant without writing a first-take receipt") addresses a *different*, narrower risk (event-coverage gaps), not this one (implied pre-cohort backstory baked into the person's generated stats). This is a genuine, unaddressed gap in the soundness argument for the one clause HIS-014 states most strictly ("Do not infer missing early-career history from an incomplete record"). It needs one of: (a) an explicit argument for why `genreExperience` does not represent implied prior credits (its type comment — `types.ts:112-114`, "per-(discipline,genre) experience... (D-9.9)" — is genuinely ambiguous on this), or (b) a documented, deliberate acceptance of the residual risk, before Mentor lands.

**2. The D5 precedence flip is presented under "Ruling 8" but is not clearly authorized by ruling 8's text — needs an explicit sign-off, not silent inclusion.**

§2.2 changes `talentMarket.ts:916-917` so `enemies here` outranks `close ties here` when both are present on a roster, citing ruling 8's "Current hostility governs current consequences even where historical friendship exists." Ruling 8's two sentences ("Current hostility governs...", "Conversely, a historical conflict alone does not override current positive closeness forever") read most naturally as a matched pair about **one pair's own current-vs-historical tier** (which the charter correctly implements elsewhere: "Tiers read current closeness and never `peakTier`"). D5 is a different mechanism entirely — a cross-counterpart studio-preference ranking, not the tier law. 647-A2's own OPEN 11 (`647-A2:39`, correctly cited) explicitly left this precedence question open, to be "decided by the slice that lands a conflict driver" — which is this slice, but that only means the *deferral condition* is met, not that ruling 8 pre-answered it.

The charter is honest about this — it labels the D5 change "(inferred; see §6 item 1)" both inline and in §6, and explicitly invites correction ("If you disagree, that sentence and `647-A2:39` are the text to quote"). That is the right way to handle genuine ambiguity, and I do not think this is an invented decision. But it is a real, observable **behavior change** (which studio a contested person prefers), not a routine implementation detail, and it is currently packaged under the "§2.2 Ruling 8, inside `tierOf`" heading alongside clauses that genuinely are ruling-8-mandated. Recommend the parent make one explicit documented decision on this single line before it lands, rather than letting it ride in under the ruling-8 heading by default.

## Non-blocking notes

- **Citation overclaim on "qualifying pictures."** §1 says `retainedTransitionEvidence`'s general acting-evidence builder (`professionTransitions.ts:54-66`, confirmed exact) "is the C3 'qualifying pictures' law (942-C3:21-33)." Checked `942-c3-execution-contract.md:21-33`: 942-C3 actually reserves the term "qualifying pictures" for its *lead-role-restricted, counterpart-specific* collaboration count ("For directing, count first takes where subject is lead and counterpart is director" — line 28), which is `professionTransitions.ts`'s separate `leads`/`directing` variable (lines 67-69), not the general `takes` variable the charter cites. The charter's actual Mentor design (any acting role, not lead-only) is a defensible product choice on its own merits — HIS-014 and companion §5.3's example don't restrict to leads — but the citation borrows a term 942-C3 defines narrower than how it's being used here. Recommend correcting the citation to present this as the charter's own interpretive choice, not an already-settled C3 rule.
- **Romance growth vs. eligibility.** Companion §5.4a's eligibility gate is "both... at Friends or above" **and** "neither is currently a Partner of anyone." The charter's §2.3 growth clause only gates on friendship tier ("Both apply only while the friendship tier is Friends or above"); the "no open bond" check is applied only at formation (§2.3 Formation; §6 item 4). This means romance value can accumulate for a pair while one person already has an open bond elsewhere, only blocking at the formation write. This is a reasonable simplification but is not the same gate companion §5.4a describes for "the track" as a whole; worth a line in §6 rather than silence.
- **Projection-57 DTO shape.** §4 describes `StudioRelationshipRow.labels: {label: 'Mentor'|'Professional Rivals', evidence: text}[]` as a single combined shape without clearly stating whether the `'Professional Rivals'` union member is introduced at projection 57 (slice A) before it can ever be emitted (Rivals needs the `competitions` log, which is slice B/Save44 only) or deferred to 58. Given this codebase's demonstrated sensitivity to premature closed-enum entries (the `priorityOrder` 44→45 precedent cited in 647-A2), recommend splitting the DTO description explicitly: 57 = `labels: {label: 'Mentor', evidence}[]` only; 58 adds `'Professional Rivals'` and `romance`.
