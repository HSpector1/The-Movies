## Outcome and authority

A retired actor can continue an appropriate new career as a director or writer,
with the same identity and complete history. Retirement from acting stays final.
Ending the last profession and leaving the industry must be distinguishable from
being an acting alumnus who now works in another profession. No card/person clone,
skill loss, invented credit, employer assignment or money accompanies a transition.

## Source facts that rule out a role-only patch

1. `careerLifecycle.ts` finds records and blocks market/admission by person alone;
   intent refuses any second record. Save validation requires one record per person
   and `record.profession === Talent.role`. A transition requires current-profession
   selection and immutable past-profession records, with at most one record per
   `(personId, profession)` under the narrow non-returning catalogue.
2. V34's cap applies every later employment interval to the first announcement.
   Later work in the new profession needs a dated profession interval. Contracts
   held at an announcement still end by its effective boundary; no past cap changes.
3. C.4 re-derives every old cohort request and entrant role from today's Talent.role.
   Rewriting it changes historical requests retroactively. Both entrant-at-entry
   and active-profession-at-request must read dated authority, never today's role.
4. `hollywoodValidation.ts` checks authored starting credits against today's primary
   role. The original role remains a historical fact even after a real transition.
   Frozen public readers must stay strict; current delegation needs explicit dated
   authority, not changed fixture bytes, invented contracts or mutable global mode.
5. Direct core greenlight admission is has-discipline, broader than UI pools. Making
   a person active again would silently reopen acting seats unless the requested
   profession is checked. New-role work must succeed; former-profession work must
   fail even through direct core commands. Permanent writer credit on a later film
   is not a new writing assignment and retains its accepted separate rule.
6. Retirement-writing validation deliberately rejects duplicate person records.
   Its current-profession resolution must be amended explicitly while retaining
   every temporal, identity and original-employment guard proven by880-B/910.
7. Current P10 `careerIdentity` reads displayed/perceived role OVR and the capability
   minimum60, with actual workHistory distinguishing proven from capable-unproven.
   Potential bands are already public. C.3 must not consume hidden ceilings or
   manufacture workHistory. The existing comment that the sim never consumes this
   summary would need an explicit P10 generalization, not silent reuse.
8. FirstTakeReceipt contains director and three actors, but no writer. B.5 edges
   likewise cover those four people; mentorship is not a current driver. Writer
   collaboration must use real released-picture credit membership, with provenance
   and missing-history limits, not a fictional writer edge/mentor label.
9. Promise evaluation runs before market settlement and lifecycle completion.
   Profession-specific eligibility must reach quote/admission/feasibility and
   VOIDED reasoning without reopening terminal outcomes or changing trust law.
10. C.2-RM currently has singular lifecycle/alumni DTOs and freezes relationship
    display as of profession retirement. A working new-profession person needs
    current relationships plus retained retirement/history facts; only final
    industry alumni use the final retirement as-of display. No raw edge rewrite.

## Reconciliation decisions before the input probe

The following are parent-selected delegated readings, pending the exact contract
review. They are recorded before940/941 measures candidates, so input output
cannot silently become its own eligibility oracle.

- A request-profession check preserves all existing has-discipline work except
  a profession the person has actually retired from; it does not make direct core
  admission primary-role-only. Finishing/inactive/final industry status still
  refuses new work globally. Existing screenplay-credit reuse remains separate.
- The one final extension belongs to a profession retirement episode: direction10
  says final retirement "from that profession." A later career gets its own one
  opportunity under unchanged law; an old case can never grant a repeat extension
  or supply the new case's outcome. The exact historical episode join is required
  before implementation, not guessed from the latest row.
- Temporarily no eligible target is a deferred evaluation, not a recorded decline.
  Only it may schedule another evaluation. An actual decline-all is terminal.
  The actor waiting horizon is75 (min actor-hard70+5 and destination-hard75);
  evaluate on actual retirement and then every52 weeks while below that horizon,
  with the hard-horizon due boundary included for finality. This does not override
  C.2 notice, extension or completion of obligations. Director/Writer have no
  onward catalogue and reach finality only on actual retirement completion.
- Unchanged career inputs never gain a better chance merely through another
  evaluation. No random chances or urgency bonus. Source reconciliation must
  establish which inputs can change while inactive; existing screenplay credits
  on later releases are not new labor and must not be assumed absent.
- P10 capability60 is required for the target. Public expected potential describes
  growth headroom, not competence: Limited upside cannot exclude a proven strong
  professional. No direct hidden-ceiling read or new OVR formula is authorized.
- Initial threshold hypotheses for the exact rule are recorded acting depth of
  at least3 genuine first-take appearances, plus appropriate target context:
  existing proven target work or repeated real target collaboration (at least2
  qualifying observations). Directing context uses director–lead work; writing
  context uses released-picture writer/actor co-credits. Authored starting credits
  remain useful factual history but are not silently converted to first takes.
  The probe reports facts independently; it neither selects these thresholds nor
  asserts a positive case. An absent natural witness is reported, not repaired by
  relabeling facts or weakening P10 capability.
- Choice compares all eligible catalogue targets using public, versioned career
  descriptors and an explicit order-independent tie/decline rule. The exact bands,
  snapshot inputs, reasons and receipt schema are still the next contract's task.
  No fake studio offer, compensation amount or issuer trust value is introduced
