## Blocking

**§5.1 validation cannot detect a tampered `ArchetypeResult`/`LensSummary`, and the charter's own justification doesn't cover the case that matters.** Item 5-7 of §5.1 check only bounds, key sets and ref-existence/watermark/week; §5.1's closing paragraph says "It never re-runs archetypes... Standing at B is gone after the freeze tick, so no replay could be exact," citing 1353-A §5.1. But none of the eight archetype predicates (1353-A §5.3 table: `artistic-voice`, `audience-institution`, `commercial-engine`, `technology-pioneer`, `talent-foundry`, `genre-specialist`, `resilient-survivor`, `awards-dynasty`) reads Standing — Standing only appears as the separate `standingAtBoundary` field. Every archetype input (films, career events, adoptions, catalogue, ranking snapshots, condition events) is drawn from roots 1353-A §4's Wave R guards explicitly pin as retained forever ("Films, credits, career events, adoptions, ranking snapshots, condition events | Referenced by stable ID," 1353-A §3). Nothing stops these from being recomputed at validate time the same way the already-adopted sibling **1356-A §5 item 5** does — "rebuild the input at the record's week from persisted facts... run the evaluator... Every stored field but `band` must match," exempting only `band` because "cash at W is not recoverable." 1359-A generalizes a narrow, single-field exemption (genuinely non-recoverable Standing) into a blanket "never re-run" over the entire manifest, without identifying why the archetype math itself can't be replayed. **Failure scenario:** a hand-edited or corrupted `official.studios[i].archetypes[j]` claiming `outcome: 'held'` for `commercial-engine` with a fabricated `qualifyingCount`/refs drawn from real, in-bounds film ids loads and validates cleanly — item 7's ref check only confirms the cited ids exist and are pre-boundary, never that the predicate over those ids actually yields "held." This is a real integrity hole in a frozen, forever-authoritative historical record, not a hypothetical. Resolution: either scope "no replay" down to `standingAtBoundary` only (matching 1356-A's pattern) and recompute everything else, or the charter must give the specific, cited reason (not Standing) why archetype/lens replay is unsafe, before RED staging locks a validator that structurally cannot catch this class of tamper.

Non-blocking companion note: the genre fallback ("genre | the concept's genre from `state.concepts`," §3.1) depends on `state.concepts`, which is not one of Wave R's five pinned roots (1353-A §4: `releasedFilms`, `hollywood.films`, both career-event roots, `theatricalRuns`, `technology.adoptions`). No current writer prunes `state.concepts` (grep, no compaction/filter call sites), so this isn't an active bug, but it is an undocumented dependency alongside a charter that elsewhere (1353-A §3) explicitly worries about compaction pressure from save size. Worth a one-line Wave R addendum, not a blocker.
