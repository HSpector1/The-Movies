## Verification of each amendment

**1. `distressWeeks` (Amendment 1).** No remaining ambiguity. The rule is now three-phase and unambiguous: the counter step computes a baseline (`prev.stage===distress ? prev.distressWeeks+1 : 0`), the transition step reads that baseline to decide which row fires, and — critically — a transition *into* distress then overrides the result to `1` (1352-F lines 9–16). I recomputed the worked example independently: weeks 1–8 negative → `lowRun=4` at week 4 fires `stable→warning`; `negativeRun=8` at week 8 fires `warning→distress`, and the override sets `distressWeeks=1` at week 8 (not the `0` the bare counter-step formula would give, which was exactly the bug I flagged). From week 8, `distressWeeks` increments normally: week `8+k` has `distressWeeks = k+1`, so `distressWeeks=26` lands at week `8+25=33`, matching the closure claim exactly. Amendment 2 (below) also removes the only other path that could set `distressWeeks` via a relapse, so the override rule now has exactly one entry point. MET WITH EVIDENCE.

**2. Recovery declines as stable does (Amendment 2).** No new unreachable or stuck state. All five states (`stable, warning, distress, recovery, closed`) remain reachable under the new table; `recovery`'s two exits (`lowRun≥4→warning`, `clearRun≥13→stable`) are mutually exclusive per Lemma A, same as every other row. I re-derived the "no single week moves a studio into distress from any stage" claim rather than trusting it: since `negativeRun` is a pure fact-counter independent of stage, a negative streak beginning in `recovery` accumulates `negativeRun` and `lowRun` in parallel; by Lemma A, `lowRun≥negativeRun` at every week, so `recovery→warning` (needs `lowRun≥4`) has *already* fired no later than the streak's 4th week — well before `negativeRun` can reach 8. So the **minimum negative-week count to relapse from `recovery` all the way back to `distress` is exactly 8**, identical to the primary `stable`-path minimum, not 8+4=12 as a naive reading might suggest. This is a genuine strengthening of the "meaningful recovery" guarantee (a single bad week in recovery no longer costs a full relapse), and I verified it rigorously rather than accepting the charter's claim on its face. MET WITH EVIDENCE.

**3. Lemma A, Lemma B, and the runway distinction (Amendment 3).** Both lemmas check out under independent proof, not just charter assertion:
- Lemma A's three parts all follow directly from the definitions: `negative→low` holds in both the `O>0` and `O≤0` branches of the `Cover` definition; `lowRun≥negativeRun` follows by induction (every week in a negative-run is, by definition, also low, so the low-run extends at least as far back); `lowRun>0 ⟺ clearRun=0` is the direct biconditional consequence of `clearRun=low?0:prev+1`.
- Lemma B's proof sketch is valid end to end: only `warning→distress` (needing `negativeRun≥8`) enters distress now that Amendment 2 removed the other path; by Lemma A a studio with `negativeRun≥4` already has `lowRun≥4` and so is already in `warning`; and `warning` cannot clear while low (Lemma A forces `clearRun=0` throughout a negative run), so the studio is still in `warning` at the 8th negative week when distress fires. This is independent of `CORPORATE_WARN_COVER_WEEKS`, exactly as claimed.
- The `economyView.ts` (D-12.16) runway citation is **exactly accurate**: I re-read the source and confirmed `runwayOf(cash, burn, rev) = floor(cash/(burn−rev))` (`economyView.ts:125-130`), which nets revenue and forecasts time-to-zero, genuinely distinct in shape from `Cover = cash/O` (a stock ratio with no revenue term, matching 1350-A's band formula).
- Given Lemma B proves the actual failure-determining threshold (`distress`, and by extension `closed`) is provably independent of the cover/runway-style term — cover only gates the advisory `warning` notice and loan eligibility, never distress or closure — I find this argument adequate. **My independent determination: this does not need to go to the Owner.** MET WITH EVIDENCE.

Pure-law level: loan eligibility now requires `max≥LOAN_AMOUNT_STEP`, so a `weeklyFixedCost=0` studio is cleanly excluded from the LOAN remedy rather than silently offered an empty, impossible range.
