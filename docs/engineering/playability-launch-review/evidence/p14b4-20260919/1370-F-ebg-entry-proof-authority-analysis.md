# EBG B episode entry proof: authority analysis r1

**Decision:** The actual scheduled cash-closed decision is explicitly required for the **writer** that sets `costCutting.since`; a positive reconstruction of that decision on every Save46 load is **not explicitly required by the governing 1363-A/F validation contract**. The accepted B-only design r2 says the validator “proves each episode from actual receipt/production/technology/market history,” which can be read as a stronger positive-origin proof but is ambiguous against 1363-A §5's closed validation list. The independent EBG r1 REFINE remains in force because the separate genuine V27 admission seam is confirmed broken. The second source-review finding should be treated as a **design-authority clarification needed**, not as an established violation of 1363-A/F. No code or heavy process was run.

Governing text: `1363-A-rival-recovery-amendment-charter.md:269–286` (§4.1) requires all three facts at the end of a scheduled `decide()` in week W: no production/run, no new greenlight/commission or drafting/rewriting, and one specified cash-closed commissioning reason. `1363-F-parent-adoption-of-the-recovery-charter.md:11–24` adopts that with amendments; P1 at :99 retains the three entry conditions. The writer's `rivalCostCuttingEntry` therefore must use actual decision facts and avoid hold/busy-writer false positives.

For persisted state, however, `1363-A:439–467` (§5) explicitly names one field and Save step, then enumerates era-46 validation: exact keys/version; `since` null or an integer from entered week to T; no production/run when set; and no later new film, hire/renewal, laboratory, technology commitment or current proposal. It does **not** list a required positive receipt or a replay of the past cash/commission decision. `1363-F` amendment 2 changes later adoption comparison to committedWeek and strict later weeks; amendment 4 explicitly strips `costCutting` in the private live-profession proof. Neither adds an origin witness. The original charter even says at `1363-A:443–445` that a termination receipt cannot distinguish cost cutting from ordinary R3 release. Thus the stronger sentence in B-only design r2 hunk 6 (`DESIGN.md:20`) needs a precise interpretation before it is used as a rejection criterion.

The present Save46 roots cannot generally prove §4.1's past decision from exact serialized state without extra authority. `since` records a week, not the decision's cash, reserve, refused package cost, slot refusal, full-index evaluations or absence of a hold at that historical instant. Finance periods aggregate movements across weeks; current cash and `nextDecisionWeek` are later state; screenplay receipts/rejections do not record every refused commission or no-op scheduled decision. A raw idle studio could set `since` to current tick and make strict `> since` exclusions vacuous without proving why it entered. This is a real limit of shape/history validation, but it is within the narrower §5 contract unless the newer design intentionally strengthens that contract. Deterministic replay from seed is not a general substitute because historical player choices and imported saves need their own authenticated action history.

**Option A (recommended for current bounded B-only slice):** version a design clarification stating that “prove each episode from history” means the exact §5/1363-F structural and strictly-later commitment proof, while the writer is responsible for the positive §4.1 decision. Then independently review that clarification and the r2 source after the V27 seam fix; add direct save tamper tests for every §5 rule. This does not waive the existing EBG r1 REFINE or assert a forged episode is impossible.

**Option B:** require positive save-load provenance for every episode. That is a new persistence rule beyond the explicit §5 list and likely needs a durable decision receipt or field recording cash/reserve, stop reason and indexed outcomes, plus validation and migration treatment. It may force a ninth production path or a schema/Save-step amendment; route it for Owner/parent authority before code.

**Option C:** attempt replay-only proof from existing state/seed. The current roots lack a complete authenticated decision/action log; this cannot be claimed exact without a separately designed replay witness. Do not infer cash closure from current low cash or a later termination receipt.
