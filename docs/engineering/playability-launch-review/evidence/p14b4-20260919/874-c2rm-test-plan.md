assignment. Only actual drafting/rewriting counts. A market/pool screenplay takes
one week: commission at E−1 completes at E, so it cannot demonstrate finishing.
Use an original screenplay whose actual due week is asserted greater than E.
`completeDueScriptWork` completes retained tasks from their due week and talent
identity; it does not require the expired contract to be renewed.

## Relationship freeze: premise and discriminating control

The current production path writes first-take/release relationship drivers before
lifecycle settlement. Cancellation writes its driver synchronously, and an actor
or director retains a busy seat until release/cancel. This supports the proposed
read-at-retirement model, but is source evidence, not an executed proof. The test
must retain the retired subject's incident edges and assert that they do not change
after lawful continued ticks and current-disclosure changes. A newly discovered
lawful post-retirement driver is a contract conflict, not permission to reconstruct
past tiers from a later compacted edge.

Drift grace is52 weeks and the return span260. A freeze assertion that compares
two nearby weeks inside grace is vacuous. Require a witnessed difference in the
core current-time tier at a later week while the projected retired tier remains
the actual retirement tier. Use a real sufficiently strong edge if available;
otherwise report the missing natural premise and add a separately labelled,
whole-save-valid synthetic strength boundary. Do not weaken the comparator.

Counterpart visibility remains the current roster predicate; it must not be
frozen to the subject's retirement week. Obtain changed disclosure by real
counterpart termination/expiry and, if necessary, later lawful rehiring. Test
just before/at/after the employment boundary, respecting the existing strict
`startWeek < readWeek` rule. Do not mutate employment ordinals manually. Freeze
does not mean the entire collaborators DTO is identical: lawful disclosure can
change, and later release credits for a retired credited writer can grow history
because a writer credit is not a binding production seat.
