# Major lessons

- Authenticate the entire source hash graph before grant; keep unknown route review, operational protection and once-only grant external to immutable config to avoid circular authority.
- Keep the qualified recorder ownership, cleanup, streams, timers and finalization unchanged; replace only exact controls bindings and the obsolete producer predicate.
- A strict producer consumer must reject duplicate fields, wrong types, false count coercions, extra output, reordered or incomplete lines, stderr and generic crashes. Three expected RED markers follow their specific assertions; all actual process exits still must be zero.
- Completion requires the actual recorder/helper/tool outcomes and exact stream/metadata identities. A recorder artifact or producer summary alone cannot certify future process exits.
- Source-only route review is not runtime qualification. Preserve original28/321 and new6/344, selected2529 and declined cache, and all performance/game/full109/P17/P18 limits.

- Record actual PID, PGID and SID separately. Direct exec preserves PID but does not create an owned group by itself. Establish the wrapper group before exec and bind actual PGID through the launch claim, tool outcome and scoped absence check; never substitute PID for PGID.
- Preserve the declined source and its exact independent STOP. A minimal fresh correction needs whole-byte inverse proof and source review, without retroactively relabeling the original or adding a runtime gate.
