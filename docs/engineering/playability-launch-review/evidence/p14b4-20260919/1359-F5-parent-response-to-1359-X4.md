# 1359-F5: parent response to 1359-X4 (G-P sends two archetypes back to retuning)

[1359-X4](1359-X4-p15c-gp-probe-results.md) ran G-P. Two evaluated archetypes are held by no studio on either seed.

## Rulings

1. **The reading.** "Held by none in any seed" means no holder in every seed. It mirrors the trigger's other half,
   "held by every studio in every seed".
   - Under that reading, `artistic-voice` and `commercial-engine` trigger.
   - `audience-institution` does not: five studios hold it on seed-b. It goes unheld on p13a, where rivals stop filming
     3-7 years after entry (1357-X).
2. **§5.5 of 1353-A returns to retuning for the two archetypes,** as 1359-A §9 orders.
   - `artistic-voice`'s count floor is met; its 25% share floor is not. seed-b's r07 holds 36 acclaimed of 404
     releases, and r06 holds 14 of 419.
   - `commercial-engine`'s hit line (90% of `baseMarketValue`) is almost never crossed by rival films: 0-2 hits for
     each of five studios that made 273-464 films.
   - §5.5 set these thresholds from the presentation's critic tiers and a pooled reach percentile (1353-A :187-191).
     The collapse alone does not explain the result, because seed-b's thriving entrants hold neither.
3. **The retune follows 1353-A's route.**
   - A tuning amendment with its own record, grounded in the measured distributions of critic score and of
     gross ÷ `baseMarketValue` for rival releases on both seeds.
   - Then an independent review, then G-P again.
   - The charter's intent stands: a pattern, not volume, so each archetype keeps a count floor and a share floor.
4. **Order.**
   - G-P gates Wave 2 production (1359-A §9), not the RED. The P15C RED proceeds on its own path: the mint at the last
     writer below the P15 step, then the recorded RED.
   - The amendment can go ahead now, in parallel. If Owner question 1357-Q1 changes the rival economy, G-P runs again
     on the changed tree before production either way.
