## R3 authority clarification: writer entry versus save admission

This section clarifies the r2 hunk 6 phrase “proves each episode from actual receipt/production/technology/market history.” Governing 1363-A §4.1 (lines 269–286) and 1363-F P1 require the **writer** to set `costCutting.since=W` only at the end of a scheduled `decide()` after observing all three actual entry conditions: no active production/run, no greenlight/commission/drafting/rewriting, and an enumerated cash-closed commissioning reason. A hold, busy writer, economic rejection, refused renewal alone, or unrelated Scientist slot does not supply a cash-closed film slot. The existing `rivalCostCuttingEntry` facts and tick call remain mandatory; this clarification does not weaken their rule or authorize a shortcut based on low current cash.

Governing 1363-A §5 (lines 439–467), as amended by 1363-F's strict later-week and committed-week rules, gives the **Save46 reader** a different closed proof obligation. It checks exact `costCutting` shape/version; a null or integer `since` between the studio's entered week and save week; no production/run while set; no forbidden film, hire/renewal, laboratory, technology, research, sound or market commitment **strictly after** `since`; lawful real termination charges and receipt/interval/account reconciliation; and the separately required zero-refund, no-disposal, standing-body and full-life opex invariants. Equality and earlier ordinary history remain legal. All checks run on the original unknown V46 input before any private older-era projection. The reader preserves a lawful non-null episode through V46 save/load/save and refuses its downgrade to V45/V19.

The exact V46 roots do not persist every transient §4.1 input: past cash and reserve at decision, refused package cost, blocked film slot, index outcomes and scheduling fact. Therefore this design does **not** require the reader to replay or positively infer the historical writer decision from current cash, `nextDecisionWeek`, termination, or a fabricated existing receipt. A raw save with an otherwise idle studio and `since` at the current tick can satisfy §5 without proving why it entered. That is an explicit limit of the adopted save contract, not evidence that the writer law was followed. Adding an authenticated entry witness would be a new persistence rule requiring a separate authority/design amendment and review; r3 does not add a receipt, schema field, ninth path, test fixture or acceptance claim.
