## Minimal evidence order

1. **Recover the missing third employment role.** The r7 historical 4cff witness source `SOURCE-PINS.json` SHA-256 `69ca5043bb533908f722126fbc93fd59ce84f0d7388180f3b60c5074076aebc2` has independent source-only ACCEPT `4f873fc7b0d0d24d0d339d1893f523a717da6bd00942a5920bc67ee659d317ff`; it has no filled binding or actual run. After an independently reviewed exact sparse materialization and sufficient disk, capture all 44 rows and the other three families/RNG in one 416-tick pipe-only run. Require its own 4cff digest before treating it as the post-aging preimage. Compare H-original→post-aging and post-aging→M0 separately by `(contractId, occurrence)`, source order, first term and lifecycle change. Preserve all three raw arrays and roles. Do not infer 4cff from 09bcc or S1.
2. **Employment one-change controls.** Existing M0→S1 exact-age input effect is already observed and should be referenced, not rerun. On a pinned predecessor adjacent to the P14C.1 aging change, isolate aging alone and record age/provenance, first offer salary and bonus, employment mutations, cash debit, all four final arrays and RNG. If the 4cff preimage still differs from the candidate, isolate retirement/cohort/renewal boundaries one at a time on their nearest compatible predecessors. A control earns a narrow attribution only if its parent differs by one reviewed semantic change and the changed full rows and unchanged families are independently checked.
3. **First-take residual control.** Retain F1 as the measured single-return film hold control. After H route clearance, first add an observation-only H commission/package/hold witness for r01 film 10 around weeks 93–114; use `NOT_PRESENT_IN_ERA` for unavailable H fields and verify H's own four raw hashes/RNG. Then compare H, M0 and F1 internal commission, greenlight and take moments. Only after the first differing internal predicate is known should a single reviewed film rule be changed on a pinned immediate predecessor. Compare all 51/53 take identities and occurrence, not only film 10 or its week.
4. **Market decision and settlement control.** Use observation-only separately pinned H/M0 hooks for r01-0, r01/r02/r03, from proposal week 196 through freeze/settlement week 208. At authoring, retain the actual candidate order, first accepted attachment, **stored week-196 feasibility** and submission quote. At freeze, retain source-order case index and the exact pre-case state/input digest, then the **fresh week-208 attached-feasibility** result separately from the stored receipt. Observe the first reached freeze predicate in actual order; later predicates are `NOT_REACHED`, and candidate evaluations not executed are `NOT_EVALUATED`. Record dropped proposal order, survivor order and chooser bands only when reached, without re-evaluating predicates. A prior week-208 case can change seats/state before this subject, so case order and pre-case inputs are essential. Each observer must reproduce its own four array digests/RNG. The first reached differing internal decision determines a one-variable follow-up: attachment/feasibility **or** chooser ranking, never both in one arm. Independently compare full receipt rows by `(week,kind,talentId,studioId,occurrence)` and settlement tuples by `(week,talentId,kind,occurrence)`. Record reasons/drops, row counts and unchanged families.
5. **Decide the ledger only after the controls.** For each transition retain source pins, independent static/observed reviews, child exit, timeout, cleanup, first changed field, matched/changed/unmatched rows, four SHA-256 arrays and RNG. If a single change moves one visible row but leaves the family digest false, report partial attribution. Interactions need a separately named combined arm. Never repin an unexplained mismatch.
