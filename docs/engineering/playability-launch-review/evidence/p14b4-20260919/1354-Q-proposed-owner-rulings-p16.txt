PROJECT: STUDIO — PROPOSED OWNER RULINGS FOR P16 AND THE P15B CLOSURE HANDOFF

DRAFT FOR HOWARD'S APPROVAL. These are recommendations until Howard explicitly
approves them. A draft is not Owner approval.

Source: the P16 research at 084713980ef8 (never Owner authority) as read in
record 1354-W0, against the adopted P15B charter (1352-A with 1352-F) and the
rulings of 2026-09-29 (1340-O, 1342-O). None of these questions is answered by
those rulings. Each item gives the recommended option first. On approval, record
them in the existing decision register and continuation records, and apply them
at the next safe checkpoint without interrupting a recorded run.

1. RIVAL CLOSURE STAYS PRIVATE UNTIL IT HAPPENS; NO RETENTION ON THE ESTATE PATH

Keep the 1122-A disclosure: rivals show their distress stage and band, never a
closure countdown. When a rival closes, its people are released to free agency
in the closure week, and a later P16 purchase of its estate buys assets only
(films, rights, facilities), not a window to keep its staff. Alternatives: a
public "closure due" notice with a bidding window, or contracts held open past
closure with wages owed. Both change an Owner presentation ruling and the wage
law, so they are not recommended for the first slice.

2. THE PLAYER'S ESTATE IS SETTLED IN THE CLOSURE WEEK

When the player's studio fails, everything settles in the closure week. People
go to free agency. Films and rights are archived as the studio's permanent
record. Facilities refund under the existing demolition law into the closure
record. No rival buys the player's estate, and the run then ends with the
recoverable end-of-run record of ruling 3. Alternative: keep the world running
until rivals buy the estate, which delays the end-of-run record.

3. A CLOSED STUDIO'S RIGHTS STAY ARCHIVED

A closed studio's story properties and film rights stay unowned and archived,
not for sale, in the first P16 slices. There is no claim or auction mechanism.
Alternative: an estate auction for rights, deferred to a later slice.

4. NO GROWTH OR ACQUISITION FINANCING

The only loan stays the P15B distress loan. Purchases are cash-funded above the
studio's reserve, and a studio with an outstanding loan cannot bid. This keeps
ruling 3's "no new undocumented debt product". Alternative: a documented growth
loan, which is a new product that would need its own charter and ruling.

5. NO DUE-DILIGENCE ACCESS TO RIVAL FINANCES

Bidders see public facts only: stage, band, released films, public rights.
Nothing reveals a rival's cash, book figures or contract terms, which keeps
ruling 2's "no private rival balances". Alternative: a costed due-diligence
event that reveals a target's figures to one bidder.

6. ONLY A STABLE STUDIO MAY SELL ITSELF ON THE HEALTHY PATH

A whole-studio sale on the healthy path needs a willing target in the stable
stage. A studio in warning, distress or recovery cannot sell itself that way.
Its route is the P15B remedies, or closure followed by an estate sale. It may
still sell single assets under P16B's ordinary rules.

7. NO SECOND BIDDER FOR ORDINARY SALES

Ordinary asset sales between two active studios have one buyer and one seller
under the sub-cost floor. Competitive bidding is used only for estate sales.

8. KEEPING STAFF AFTER A HEALTHY ACQUISITION

When the player buys a studio and takes no action in the retention window,
every contract that fits the player's usable capacity continues, in the
target's contract order. The rest go to free agency. Keeping staff is a
priority opportunity, never ownership of people.

9. RESEARCH DEFAULTS FOR THE REMAINING DETAILS

Adopt the research defaults for the lower-priority items:
- in-flight research of an acquired studio cancels, and its verified partial
  progress survives;
- an inventor's price advantage does not transfer;
- licences have no territory sub-split in the first slices;
- the Star & Script Selling Facility stays outside P16B until separately
  dispositioned.

Delegated, not asked: when a StoryProperty is created (recommended at
Greenlight), the library valuation formula, the P16A save root and the Licence
fields. These are written down and reviewed before implementation, as with
earlier delegated tuning.
