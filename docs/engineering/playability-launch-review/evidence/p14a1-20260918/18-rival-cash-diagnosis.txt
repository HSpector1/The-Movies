═════ CAUSE (one paragraph) ═════
The rival cash collapse the re-expression attributed to the market is INHERITED and
pre-market. At ef9ff76, with no talent market in the build at all, r03 is already
-14,726,582 and r04 -9,584,173 at week 416, both at roster 0, and r02 already caps
near 4.4M; under the market those become -16,239,214, -7,795,398 and 3,900,619 —
r03 1.5M worse, r04 1.8M BETTER, r02 0.5M worse. The market did not create that
trajectory and does not meaningfully deepen it; the P13B-S8 rival economy already
ran r03 and r04 into permanent insolvency on this seed. What the market genuinely
changed is one 348,487-dollar margin: a settlement re-signs a rival's own person at
a PREMIUM TIER instead of staff()'s plain ask, which cost r02 419,824 of extra
payroll and 93,490 of extra bonuses by week 416 and raised its operating reserve by
87,084 — enough to flip its +251,911 slack at the week-416 gate to -348,487, so the
first commercial synchronized-sound purchase falls from r02 at week 416 to the
later-entering r05 at week 520. That is GENUINE BEHAVIOUR under the pinned
settlement law and the OPEN rival tier hypothesis: a contested re-signing is
supposed to cost more than an uncontested renewal, and r02 was 251,911 from the gate
before the market existed.

═════ THE ONE DEFECT, AND ITS EXACT SITE ═════
Separately from the cash question, the measurements expose a roster the law does not
intend. `commitRivalWinner` (src/core/talentMarket.ts) appends the winner's
employment row and pushes its ordinal onto activeEmploymentOrdinals with NO seat
budget, and nothing ever releases the surplus, so a rival that wins challenger cases
grows past RIVAL_TEAM_ROLES without bound: r01 goes 6 -> 12 (week 208) -> 16 (320)
-> 28 (420), paying payroll on 28 people for a six-role team forever, which more
than doubles its operating reserve (2,184,598 -> 4,625,088).
The enabling predicate is `rivalProposalTrigger` branch (c),
  const heldAtEffectiveWeek = sameRole.filter(e => e.terms.endWeekExclusive > descriptor.decisionWeek).length
  return heldAtEffectiveWeek < required
At a SYNCHRONIZED expiry week — which is exactly what a founding cohort signed at
week 0 on identical 208-week terms produces — every one of the studio's own rows has
endWeekExclusive EQUAL to the decision week, so the filter counts zero held for every
role and branch (c) fires for every case in the industry, even though that studio's
own settlements are about to refill those very seats. Every rival therefore bids on
every person, and whoever wins keeps them all on top of its retained roster. No fix
proposed here.
