**Structural finding for N7:** there are two independent ledgers — `state.studioEvents` (operations; Tier D permanent + Tier W 26-week) and `state.studioHistory` (narrative; permanent). `bridge/history.ts` publishes only the second, so every operations-side outcome (queue admission, phase entry, scenery arrival, reservation grant/release) is *stored but unreachable by the client*. One read-only `operationsEventsProjection` closes most of N7's retrievability gap without touching the sim. The only genuine sim dependencies are contract lifecycle and casting-review completion.
