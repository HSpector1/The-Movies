## 3. Preserved player fantasy

The player must be able to name the rival studio fought for decades, the Star lost to it, the rival
film that changed the market, the studio that rose, declined, recovered, or closed, and the
competitor whose identity persisted across the campaign. Hollywood outside the gate continues
when unobserved, consumes conserved resources, employs persistent people, creates persistent
films, develops recognizable strategies, leaves durable history, and exposes facts rather than
invented presentation.

and industry weather; studios, people, films, rivalries, and dynasties are fictional. The authored

unapproved separate mode choice.

### P08 history arrangement

Three arrangements remain visible until final P08 reconciliation:

- **A — one shared history root:** simple query surface but risks transferring P08 ownership and
  rewriting accepted player history.
- **B — player Studio History plus a P12 industry-event ledger:** preserves domain ownership and
  the Package 12 source recommendation, but needs a merged read model for cross-studio browsing.
- **C — independent domain logs with a projection-only merge:** strongest ownership isolation but
  can become adapter-heavy and inconsistently paged.

**Recommendation:** B with C's projection discipline. Keep final P08 player Studio History intact;
P12 owns a typed industry receipt ledger for studio/rival facts; a bounded TypeScript read model
merges only adapted facts for the Industry surface. An unadapted domain stays visibly incomplete.
Do not finalize event shapes, cursors, or route ownership until the final P08 TS/Unity/schema/save
identities replace the placeholders. The refresh trigger is P08 technical seal plus Owner acceptance
and publication of its final producer/consumer handoff.

### Player-lot detail versus rival abstract capacity

Player facilities, placement, routes, and physical bodies remain P09 truth. A rival can instead own
authoritative development, casting, stage, scenery, post, and management-throughput capacity.
Every unit is persisted or deterministically derived from an explainable endowment/investment;
reserved and released by exact project transitions; constrained by cost and elapsed time; and later
filtered through P13 technology/era compatibility. A missing unit must delay, repackage, cancel, or
refuse a project. Off-screen is never free.

### P10 person/employment separation

`PERSON` is one persistent industry identity. `EMPLOYMENT` is a dated relationship between that
person and one studio. `CREDIT` is historical work attribution. `PRESENCE` is current physical or
nonphysical location. `AVAILABILITY` is whether a legal assignment can use the person. These facts
may differ and must never be collapsed.

### P11 internal versus visible rival finance

Internal authority must conserve exact current cash, committed obligations, payroll, ordinary
overhead, capacity/facility Opex, development/production/post/marketing commitments, capital
actions, release receipts, and auditable period reconciliation. A compact rival account may retain
material rows plus annual category summaries; it need not clone the player's permanent weekly
ledger. At every audit boundary, opening cash + inflows − outflows = closing cash.

Player-visible information is narrower: public films, credits, release timing, public Standing, and
later lawfully disclosed condition bands. Exact rival cash, runway, salary, clauses, reserves,
forecast, policy coefficients, internal quality, and private RNG remain hidden. Storage does not
grant visibility.

## 11. Genuine Owner decision

**DECISION:** For an existing pre-P12 save, are the deterministic initial rivals known current
industry participants whose earlier detailed history is unavailable, or studios newly entering at
the migration week?

**WHY IT MATTERS:** The alternatives create different founding/entry events and therefore different
historical truth for P08 History, Hollywood Wire, P15 entrant/Legacy interpretation, and save copy.

**OPTION A:** Known current participants. Founding and entry remain unknown; the exact industry
recording boundary is stored and no synthetic entry event is emitted.

**OPTION B:** New entrants. Entry week equals migration week and each studio receives a genuine
typed entry event, with no claimed pre-entry history.

**RECOMMENDATION:** Option A. It adds present simulation truth without asserting that conversion
caused a simultaneous Hollywood founding wave.

**DEFAULT IF DEFERRED:** Preserve the data distinction and use Option A's neutral `record available
since` copy; block `founded`/`entered` language and entry-event production until ruled.

**DEPENDENT PACKAGES:** P08 adapters; P12A migration/history; Hollywood Wire; P15B entry; P15C
Legacy; P16 ownership history.

No other new Owner decision is required for P12A pre-readiness. Rival count, immutable identity,
player naming, minimum labor seam, fictional-Hollywood direction, campaign horizon, no Power
Ranking, and advanced-labor exclusion are already resolved. Exact type names, file owners, version
numbers, paging shape, tuning, and route placement are implementation-recon engineering choices.
