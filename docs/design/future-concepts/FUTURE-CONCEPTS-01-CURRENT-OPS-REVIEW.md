## 2. Reconciliation with existing product law

### No duplicate quality system

None of the concepts may create a universal film score, hidden “good taste” value, bravery bonus, constraint bonus, location bonus, department-head bonus, or scenario score. P03/P04 already separate creative promise and role Fit from OVR; P10 separates creative persona from mood and skill. Accepted core law requires exact deterministic state, while P12 product direction additionally conserves rival projects when a rival simulation is present. New decisions must flow through exact resources, capability, availability, schedule, cost, risk, observable history, and authored objectives rather than fake summaries.

### No duplicate world authority

- LOC extends P05’s physical requirement law; it does not replace Stage/Set authority. An access agreement is not a P09 property and not a P16 story right.
- SCN packages a starting state and rules; it does not write a parallel simulation, invent history, or reinterpret migrated saves.
- CRE constrains existing creative and production facts; it does not displace `FilmShape`, `Promise`, Fit, persona, the owning people/career domains, Film Library, or StoryProperty.
- DEL calls the same legal commands as the player under narrower authority; it does not own a second wallet, bypass P11 economics, or fabricate outcomes.

### No fake history

P08’s sparse ledger rule governs all four concepts. Unknown old state stays unknown. Authored scenario premises, simulated prehistory, imported actual history, and newly simulated events require distinguishable provenance. Location and delegate receipts record facts that actually happened. Constraint identity derives only from credited, observed work. No feature backfills unrecorded films, relationships, locations, or decisions.

### Anti-tedium remains a gate

The test is not “more simulation.” Every recurring interaction must earn its attention cost through meaningful workload, legibility, or capability. Ordinary work stays autonomous. Notifications aggregate and remain configurable. Repeated confirmations, weekly department clicks, routine star dragging, constant weather babysitting, per-scene logistics, and mandatory meeting loops are excluded.

All four concepts must consume the existing attention-aggregation authority rather than aggregate independently. One causal event becomes one decision packet with cross-feature consequences, at most one blocking prompt, shared severity/urgency/actionability, cooldown/digest behavior, and drill-down. Receipts, History, Wire, and Radio may retain/report owning facts without generating duplicate interruptions. A future combined LOC+CRE+DEL fixture inside SCN must count prompts and prove this composition before the concepts can safely coexist.

## 5. Stable discovery requirements

These IDs stabilize this research set only. They are not accepted schema or implementation requirements.

### LOC requirements

- **LOC-REQ-001:** Offer Stage, backlot, nearby practical, distant practical, and mixed plans only where the scene group is eligible.
- **LOC-REQ-002:** Before commitment, show a factual dossier: scenes/look served, window, travel, people/equipment, support needs, known conditions, mitigations, fallbacks, and consequence ranges with reasons.
- **LOC-REQ-003:** Commit one idempotent plan operation that atomically revalidates and establishes exact people/equipment/space/time reservations, charges each owning cost once, prevents double booking, and emits one receipt; payroll is counted once and only incremental location/logistics costs are additional.
- **LOC-REQ-004:** Extend—not replace—P05 physical-requirement satisfaction: secured temporal access may satisfy an eligible requirement; stage-only work remains stage-only; mixed work reserves both footprints.
- **LOC-REQ-005:** Make weather, noise, access, daylight, preparation, fallback, and recovery legible; never convert them into a generic location bonus.
- **LOC-REQ-006:** Record truthful departure, exception, return, and location facts through existing history/event authority.
- **LOC-REQ-007:** Keep the studio lot the home space; any away presentation is bounded and optional, not a second lot or open world.
- **LOC-REQ-008:** Keep temporal `LocationAccess` conceptually distinct from P09 property/facility authority and P16 story rights; the final type/name is unapproved.
- **LOC-REQ-009:** Resolve outcomes through exact fit, capability, resources, time, cost, and risk—never a universal quality score or guaranteed prestige.
- **LOC-REQ-010:** Delays extend the real absence/reservation consequences and disclose uncertainty; no fake exact ETA.

### SCN requirements

- **SCN-REQ-001:** Keep authored takeover, shared starting challenge, and actual-save handoff as three distinct modes.
- **SCN-REQ-002:** Label provenance as authored premise, simulated prehistory, imported actual history, or unknown/unrecorded.
- **SCN-REQ-003:** Carry and validate an explicit compatibility tuple: challenge/revision, snapshot hash, simulation/content/save and effective economy versions, rules/difficulty/configuration, assists/delegation, mods, and RNG algorithm/stream schema.
- **SCN-REQ-004:** Never invent films, transactions, people histories, or continuity to make a starting state look complete.
- **SCN-REQ-005:** Preserve the source by copy-on-import; isolate run/profile/rewards; sanitize files and metadata; retain origin only with explicit consent.
- **SCN-REQ-006:** Show premise, starting liabilities/assets, rule deltas, goals, failure terms, recovery paths, and whether play may continue after failure; every economic summary is a projection of the validated snapshot, never a second editable balance sheet.
- **SCN-REQ-007:** Compare independently disclosed outcomes such as objective state, elapsed weeks, films, finances, or careers; do not collapse them into a universal score.
- **SCN-REQ-008:** Prototype offline and local-first; accounts, workshop, cloud discovery, comments, and moderation remain separate investments.
- **SCN-REQ-009:** Describe fairness as the same ex-ante package, not identical later luck; transition comparison eligibility monotonically with reasoned receipts and disclose neutral configuration, retry, mod, migration, and continuation facets without penalizing non-simulation accessibility settings.
- **SCN-REQ-010:** Keep challenge achievement/history separate from main-profile progression until explicitly approved.

### CRE requirements

- **CRE-REQ-001:** Reuse Shape, Promise, Fit, credited roles, persona, resources, and history; add no duplicate taste or quality axis.
- **CRE-REQ-002:** Every constraint is either substantive and mechanically enforced or clearly descriptive; never imply enforcement that does not exist.
- **CRE-REQ-003:** Do not reward bravery, austerity, novelty, conventionality, or rule count by default.
- **CRE-REQ-004:** Express tradeoffs through exact capability, talent, resource, schedule, budget, availability, and disclosed risk facts.
- **CRE-REQ-005:** Preview overlap, conflicts, redundancy, exploitation, and exit consequences before commitment.
- **CRE-REQ-006:** Derive identity only from fulfilled, credited, observed choices over a disclosed sample/window; use tendency language, allow mixed bodies of work, avoid stereotypes, and add no progress/stability meter or reward.
- **CRE-REQ-007:** Preserve the people split: P10 person/profile/contract and credited-career truth, P12 employer/interval mutation, and P14 proposals, explicit preferences, promise/trust, relationships, and lifecycle facts; infer none from outcomes.
- **CRE-REQ-008:** Never use hidden skill, ceilings, demographics, or protected traits to label creative identity.
- **CRE-REQ-009:** Leave Film Library/StoryProperty identity to P16 and continuation/franchise/Story DNA to P17.
- **CRE-REQ-010:** Treat Advanced Movie Maker or shot-level authorship as a separate optional investment, not an implied dependency.

### DEL requirements

- **DEL-REQ-001:** Distinguish Inform, Prepare, and Act-within-limits; make Escalate the visible out-of-bounds behavior attached to each disposition rather than a higher-power tier.
- **DEL-REQ-002:** Keep baseline usability and ordinary autonomous work available without hiring a named head; provide safe presets and progressive disclosure so configuring a mandate does not cost more attention than the work it saves.
- **DEL-REQ-003:** A mandate states issuer, delegate, allowed action/subject, per-action cap, aggregate cap/window, optional cash floor, start/expiry, revocation state, and escalation policy.
- **DEL-REQ-004:** Delegated work invokes the same legal command and final validation as manual work; a mandate can narrow authority but never widen it.
- **DEL-REQ-005:** Bind an intent to one mandate revision and atomically commit the legal command effect, cash/resource consequence, one usage increment, and its receipt; failure changes none of them, while idempotent IDs and final revalidation handle retries and races.
- **DEL-REQ-006:** Revocation affects future acts, expiry survives save/load correctly, and already committed legal acts are not silently rewritten.
- **DEL-REQ-007:** Delegation adds no hidden outcomes, passive bonuses, second simulation, or free-form model authority.
- **DEL-REQ-008:** Greenlight, cancellation, release, final casting/hiring/firing, debt, rights/contracts, build/demolish, screenplay, Shape, and Promise remain player decisions by default.
- **DEL-REQ-009:** Recommendations and refusals explain facts, consequences, used/remaining authority, and a clear override or escalation path.
- **DEL-REQ-010:** Named department heads are a later character/voice/prioritization layer under the P10/P12/P14 people/career split; they cannot invent a profession merely to gate controls.

## 8. Contradictions and unresolved tensions

- The Blueprint preserves an open-ended single timeline and distinguishes Sandbox; SCN adds authored starts. Resolution: keep challenge runs as explicit modes and never turn the main timeline into mission progression.
- The original game’s “locations” are physical on-lot Sets. LOC uses the industry meaning of off-lot filming. Resolution: terminology and requirement substitution must be explicit; no retroactive redefinition.
- Delegation promises fewer clicks but can create a policy-management game. Resolution: few high-level, persistent mandates; aggregated exception handling; no weekly renewal ritual.
- Constraints can strengthen authored identity but easily become a solved meta or moral ranking. Resolution: exact situational consequences, no intrinsic bonus, and identity as observation rather than verdict.
- Fair starts conflict with a living, versioned simulation. Resolution: pin and disclose the ex-ante package; distinguish migrated/modded/assisted runs rather than promising timeless equivalence.
- Rich away filming could compete with the lot as the core place. Resolution: the lot remains home; off-lot is a bounded production excursion unless separately approved as a major product investment.

## 10. Research limitations and confidence

- **High confidence:** accepted-base and branch facts, current schema absence, the documented P05/P06/P08–P17 allocations with authority tier as recorded in §1, and original-game on-lot physical-set behavior. These are supported by exact repository refs and primary/local sources.
- **Medium–high confidence:** production/logistics role patterns, save provenance/security needs, and bounded-authorization safety principles. Sources are authoritative, but abstractions vary by era, jurisdiction, and software domain.
- **Medium confidence:** player value and anti-tedium judgments. Shipped comparators demonstrate feasible patterns, not that the translation will be enjoyable in Project: Studio.
- **Low / deliberately unresolved:** formulas, balance values, profession counts, UI layout, era unlock dates, content volume, networking, moderation, and the value of a navigable away-space. No research finding authorizes these.

Evidence is concentrated in English-language US/UK/European sources and selected shipped strategy/management games. Film practices differ across production scale, jurisdiction, period, labor regime, and culture. Public materials often describe notable productions, which creates survivorship bias. No player study, prototype test, accessibility study, production estimate, legal review, privacy review, or security threat model was conducted.
