## Material findings and corrections

- **Capacity is not standard.** The baseline office supplies two shared screenplay/audition/production Development slots. Annex/Hall add one/two. Office II/III are separate effect-only buildings with no slots; the highest operational standard wins studio-wide.
- **Increment is not headline.** II→III changes strength input +4→+9, a nominal +5, subject to the 100 cap. Original-writing richness rises one term before writer effects/rounding/clamping, not necessarily one displayed week. Strength is evaluated at first-draft completion; the richness tier is retained from mint, while writer-pool changes can recompute duration under existing law. No actor-skill or audition bonus is supplied.
- **Counts need physical identities.** Accepted founding property already links bodies to providers. Production/Post houses two providers; Casting/Talent is a separate body with none. Operations alone cannot count buildings. Placements, provider resources, slots and mounted Sets are different subjects.
- **Finance reuse needs a route adapter.** Delivered Finance lists placed-facility costs and opens exact history. It does not already offer all-owned inventory or renovation details. Its current/later cash and Opex semantics are reusable; history's aggregate Opex cannot be reconstructed into invented per-building payments.
- **P13 has a real overlap.** Its proposed `AdoptionWorkOrder` has its own work/progress/reservations. The candidate makes physical conversion use one P09 job with P13 participant/completion links. This is an explicit interface refinement for disposition, not an already-delivered API.
- **Cancellation differs by scope.** Recommend no voluntary abort after committing the first ordinary P09 retrofit. P13 already promises adoption cancellation; technology conversions must honor that with atomic physical/financial/technology dispositions. The ordinary recommendation cannot repeal it.

Two economic warnings are findings, not blockers invented for P12: a cheap, fast retrofit may dominate a new Annex unless disruption matters; extra Development throughput may only move the queue to actors, Sets, Stages or Post. The addendum's six scenarios expose those weaknesses without claiming enjoyment or ROI.
