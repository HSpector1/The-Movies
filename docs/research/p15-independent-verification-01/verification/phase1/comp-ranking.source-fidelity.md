**Verifier:** adversarial, lens = SOURCE FIDELITY (does the cited source actually say it; is the page/line/URL right; is the evidence tier correct)
**Date:** 2026-09-11
**Method:** every check below was reproduced by me — `sed`/`grep` on the author's saved copies in `scratchpad/web/` and `scratchpad/webcache/` (byte-compared against a fresh fetch where a URL was cited), `pdftotext` on the Prima PDF for page numbers, the accepted snapshot `scratchpad/accepted-592e926/` for code, and fresh `WebFetch`/`curl` for Steam/wiki/store pages. Nothing under any git repository was touched; no player data opened. Working files for this memo live only in `out/phase1-verify/_*.{html,cc,txt}`.

**Verdict: REFUTED IN PART.** The load-bearing spine (OpenTTD source, Simutrans source, GearCity wiki, Prima weights/scale, current-code observations) reproduces exactly, with correct line numbers. But three secondary comparators carry quotations that do not appear in any cited source, one feature is presented as base behaviour when it is an optional DLC setting, and one "developer statement" is a player's second-hand relay. The synthesis lessons survive; the confidence tiers on Hollywood Animal, Capitalism Lab (bankruptcy offer) and Transport Fever 2 must be lowered, and several page/attribution slips need correcting.

## 2. What is refuted or weak, and the corrected statement

1. **Hollywood Animal reputation "gates loans/deals" / "earned from critics' reviews" / "vital resource" (§1.5; key finding 11).** None of the six cited pages contains these phrases (grep of TheGamer, Steam store, weappy-studio.com, neonlightsmedia, game8, outsidergaming). Corrected: *Reputation rises with box-office success and "can also be spent like a currency to cover your tracks and save movies from negative attention" (TheGamer, Zoë J. Osik, 18 Apr 2025); Influence is a spendable point pool (Outsider Gaming); no inspected source establishes that reputation gates loans or deals, or that critics' reviews feed it.* Tier: press/guide, MEDIUM for the spendable-currency fact, LOW for anything else.

2. **Capitalism Lab bankrupt-firm modal offer presented as base behaviour with a "discounted price" quote (§3.3, §4.2, table 4.5; key finding 7).** The page says the offer exists "With the Acquire Companies Facing Bankruptcy setting enabled … It is a new-game setting", under the **Banking and Finance DLC**; the quoted "discounted price, reflecting its distressed financial condition" is not on the page (it says "Distressed companies are cheap"). Corrected: *With the Banking & Finance DLC's optional "Acquire Companies Facing Bankruptcy" new-game setting, a window asks whether you want to acquire the failing company; clicking Yes pauses the game for evaluation via Corporate Details → [Acquire Company].* Tier: OFFICIAL but DLC-optional, MEDIUM. The "pause only on decision-bearing events" lesson still stands on this evidence.

3. **Transport Fever 2 "developer/community-manager statement", HIGH (§1.9, §3.5, key finding 13).** The Steam post is by an unbadged player relaying "Community Manager said there will be no competitors AI…"; "boring" and "work against the player" are not in the thread. Corrected: *TF2 ships with no AI competitors (product fact); a player in the 28 May 2020 Steam thread reports second-hand that the community manager attributed this to effort comparable to multiplayer; the thread's OP calls the lack of competitors "a deal breaker" and says the game "has no competitive objective".* Tier: COMMUNITY INFERENCE, LOW–MEDIUM. The synthesis sentence "Transport Fever 2 … players call the result boring" should be reworded to the OP's actual words or dropped.

4. **Prima page for the Awards factor (§0 row 3; §7).** "Awards: 14% of Studio Rating … at the most recent award ceremony … at least six awards" is on printed **p. 51** (PDF p. 52), not p. 46. Content correct.

5. **Manual "right-click reveals a breakdown" (§0 row 4).** The manual sentence concerns Stars, not the studio chart. Per-category studio transparency rests on Prima p. 46 ("how it's doing in each of the scoring categories") — still HIGH — and the manual's separate factor list at lines 108–121 (which names "cash balance") is the better manual corroboration.

6. **Simutrans tie-break (§1.2).** Code breaks ties on `years_back − 1` (the more recent year; comment "tie breaker: next year"), then player number — not "the prior year".

7. **GearCity precision (§2.2, §3.2).** Acquisition formula quoted is the `EPS_Year ≥ −0.5` branch, not "positive EPS"; delisting requires price < 1 **and** shares/2 ≤ 1000. GearCity also has a "Networth" report and a "Prestige/Image" report (gui_reports) that the report does not mention — a relevant omission for topic 2 and for the "prestige" question.

8. **MGT2 truncated quote (§1.6).** Full sentence ends "…doesn't really matter **unless you make it matter**" — the player is saying the number is optional to care about, which slightly softens the "dismissed" framing.

9. **OpenTTD "Consequence of rank: Titles only" (table 1.11).** `company_cmd.cpp` 769–795 uses `old_economy[1].performance_history` to decide which company is offered a bankrupt rival first — a real mechanical consequence of the rating.

10. **GearCity 2014 quote lacks its EA-era caveat (§3.2).** Eric.B's "bought out or went bankrupt" is prefaced by "the horrible balancing 1980 has right now" (6 Jun 2014); the "Demand doesn't change" line is from a different 2018 thread.
