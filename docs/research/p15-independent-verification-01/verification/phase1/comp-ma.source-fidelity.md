## 2. Claims that are weak, mis-tiered, or overstated

1. **GearCity "other companies' shares vote no" — wrong source tier.** Credited to the official wiki (OFFICIAL, HIGH); the wiki (`gamemanual:howto_stockmarket`, raw export lines 204–210) does not contain it. It is Eric.B's Steam reply of 28 Feb 2021. Corrected: *DEV STATEMENT, HIGH*. The 50 %-yes, own-shares-yes, health-dependence and next-turn lockout rules **are** on the wiki. Also the report's wiki id lacks the `gamemanual:` namespace (bare id returns "This topic does not exist yet").
2. **GearCity takeover formula — over-simplified.** "1.1× evaluation / 1.2× evaluation" misreads `gm_stocks` §Takeover Price: the evaluation term is divided by 5 (unprofitable) or 2.5 (profitable), i.e. 0.22× / 0.48× of evaluation, plus a per-share premium of 1.3× / 1.75× share price, then × (1 + EPS_Year/10) and × difficulty. The report flags MEDIUM and asks for a re-read (§8 #1); this memo supplies it.
3. **Capitalism Lab "after hostile takeover or bankruptcy the company keeps running under its new owner" — half wrong.** Official page: takeover → company continues AI-run; **bankruptcy → "The company is still liquidated to pay off its debts."** The continuation-after-loss pattern the report leans on in §5 (4) and §6.2 is real but is a *takeover* pattern; the bankruptcy path is liquidation-plus-personal-survival. Also the feature is the Billionaire Life DLC, not base game.
4. **Mad Games Tycoon 2 criticism dated "Feb 2023" — it is 20–22 Feb 2024** (thread "Buying out competitors - Is it worth it?"). Quotes are accurate. A Jan 2025 thread contains a contrary Legendary-difficulty report of profitable subsidiaries; the report presents the criticism as consensus ("widely judged"), which is stronger than the evidence.
5. **Moviehouse "professional reviews say it trivialised the money loop" — inference presented as the reviewers' judgment.** Both reviews describe the passive income neutrally ("You'll never have to worry about money again"; "far exceeds any costs you'd ever accrue"). The design conclusion is reasonable; the attribution should be softened to "reviews describe".
6. **GearCity Daimler–MG "for far less than the player's quoted price"** — in the cited thread the developer traced that gap to a newspaper display bug (real price $754–828 M vs $170 M shown). AI↔AI acquisition is confirmed; the price-gap gloss is not evidence of an AI discount.
7. **Railway Empire specifics tiered "PRO/COMMUNITY"** — the gamepressure (PRO) guide only establishes "buy 100 %, then take over the assets; price rises". Merge-vs-keep-operating, keep/demolish, 10 % premium and ~150 % are Steam-thread (COMMUNITY) only. MEDIUM confidence is still fair.
8. **OpenTTD replacement attribution** — the hostile-takeover replacement shipped via PR **#10914**; #10709 only removed shares ("Bankruptcy is still in there, of course"). Changelog line 166 cites both. The "14+ issues / 16+ fix attempts" figure is loose (the maintainer's comment links 18 issues and 11 PRs).
9. **Trivial precision slips:** `delete c` is at `economy.cpp` 2005 (report: 2004); Prima "Build in Debt" entries span lines 801–1606 (report: ~769–1229); the OpenTTD "immediately if no value at month 6" branch is dead code (value floors at 1) though the source comment says it.
10. **Not reproduced (no refutation):** Software Inc. Steam guide id=2941566335 and the 2017/2023 Steam threads (Steam rate-limit + exhausted search budget). They remain COMMUNITY/MEDIUM as tagged. The official wiki's empty "Hostile takeover" / "Subsidiary/bankrupt" sections are confirmed.

## 3. Missing items the report should have caught

- **Capitalism Lab has an official bankruptcy-acquisition prompt** ("Acquire Companies That Are Facing Bankruptcy", Banking and Finance DLC game setting): "a window asks whether you are interested in acquiring the failing company"; "distressed companies are cheap"; acquisition is by buying all its shares from the Corporate Details screen. This is a shipped instance of the report's own Stage 1 shape (distress → prompt → whole-company at a discount) and belongs in the synthesis table and §6.1 "Borrowed from" column. Its absence weakens the claim that only OpenTTD/GearCity model the bankruptcy first-refusal.
- The Capitalism Lab profile should separate **takeover continuation** from **bankruptcy liquidation** (see caveat 3); §5 (4) and §6.2 currently cite it as if both paths continue the company.
- OpenTTD §1.4 should credit **#10914** for the hostile-takeover feature.
- Software Inc. and Railway Empire rows should say "specifics community-only" explicitly, since the official sources for both are silent on the mechanics the table relies on.

## 5. Verdict

**VERIFIED WITH CAVEATS.** The report's evidence base is unusually reproducible (source code, official wikis, manual full text, named developer posts) and its tiering is honest in most places. Corrections needed before it is relied on: fix the GearCity vote-rule attribution and formula, split Capitalism Lab's takeover vs. bankruptcy outcomes, correct the MGT2 date, soften the Moviehouse framing, credit #10914, and add Capitalism Lab's official bankruptcy-acquisition prompt to the Stage 1 comparator set. None of the corrections changes the synthesis or the staged recommendation; the Stage 1 recommendation is if anything *better* supported once the Capitalism Lab prompt is included.
