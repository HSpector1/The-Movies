# Adversarial Verification — `phase2/loans.md` — LENS: Authority & Direction Consistency

**Verifier role:** independent adversarial reviewer, READ-ONLY. Not the analysis's author. Checks the loan/debt design document against Owner Direction (settled 2026-09-11), accepted-code facts at 592e926, package-ownership law, and source discipline. Implements nothing.

**Verdict: VERIFIED WITH CAVEATS.** The document's core recommendation (two products; P11 mechanics + P15B condition/remedy consumption; no new P15D) is sound, well-sourced, and does not reopen any settled Direction item. It correctly and repeatedly applies "SUPERSEDED BY OWNER DIRECTION" to `P11-REQ-041`, `D-16` R10, and `D-17B`'s "next charter" clause. But it has one significant, well-evidenced authority-labeling lapse (below) and one lesser confidence-calibration inconsistency. Neither invalidates the design; both are documentation/labeling defects that a builder reading only this document would not catch.

## Finding 1 (HIGH) — `P15-PACKAGE.md §16`'s "not implied" clause is quoted as approving authority, twice, without ever being labeled SUPERSEDED

**What the document does.** `loans.md` §3.3 and §7 both quote the same sentence from `P15-PACKAGE.md` §16 as the authorizing basis for building the "Rescue" loan as a P15B "typed remedy capability family," and §7's closing paragraph states flatly: "reusing package boundaries **the Owner has already approved** (`P15-PACKAGE.md` §3.2, **§16**)."

**What §16 actually says (verified against `scratchpad/authority/P15-PACKAGE.md:600-620`), in full:**

> "Distress must have at least two legitimate recovery routes—such as reducing future obligations, delaying/cancelling an uncommitted plan, completing a conserved release, or entering dormancy—before it may demand attention. Each route belongs to a typed remedy capability family with the same eligibility predicate, conserved cost, timing, and state effect for player and rival studios; P12 rival policy chooses from that same legal set. **Loans, bailouts, investors, forced sales, or acquisition are not implied.**"

`loans.md`'s quotation in both places stops exactly at "...for player and rival studios" — immediately before the semicolon and the disqualifying final sentence. This is not an incidental omission: it is the one sentence in the entire package that speaks directly, by name, to the very mechanism ("loans") the document is designing, and it says the opposite of what the document uses the surrounding text to justify.

**Why this matters under the assigned lens.** The task's own method rule is explicit: "Distinguish CONFIRMED / QUALIFIED / CORRECTED / SUPERSEDED BY OWNER DIRECTION when you touch prior P15 claims." `loans.md` does apply this discipline carefully to `P11-REQ-041`, `D-16` R10, and `D-17B` in its §0 — but never once applies it to `P15-PACKAGE.md §16`, despite citing that exact section as approving authority in two other places. The gap is not hypothetical: the document's own primary listed evidence source, `code-finance.md` §7 (which `loans.md`'s header names as one of its inputs), already logged this exact sentence as still-live prior-P15 law as of the accepted code snapshot: *"**CONFIRMED** — 'Loans, bailouts, investors, forced sales, or acquisition are not implied' (`P15-PACKAGE.md:619`); none exists and all are Owner-blocked."* Direction F (2026-09-11) supersedes the "loans" clause of that sentence specifically. `loans.md` needed to say so explicitly, in the same place it relies on the sentence's surrounding text — it does not.

**Compounding nuance the document also misses:** the "not implied" sentence bundles five items — loans, bailouts, investors, forced sales, acquisition. Direction F only clearly authorizes the first (a "simple" loan system); Direction F itself keeps "advanced corporate finance" (investors, presumably bailouts) at P16+, and forced sales/acquisition are Direction I's separate, still-open research question (owned by `ma-auction.md`, not this document). So the correct treatment is a **partial** supersession — "loans" CORRECTED/SUPERSEDED, the other four items in the same sentence CONFIRMED as still excluded from P15 — not a blanket one. `loans.md` never draws this line, which leaves a live ambiguity: is the "Rescue" loan (Tier D) itself arguably a "bailout" under the surviving four-fifths of the sentence? The document implicitly answers "no" (it is priced, interest-bearing, and repaid, unlike a bailout) but never makes that argument, so a builder cannot tell whether the document considered the question or simply didn't see the sentence.

**Corrected statement (what the document should say, e.g. appended to §0 or footnoted at both §3.3/§7 citations):**

> "`P15-PACKAGE.md` §16's sentence 'Loans, bailouts, investors, forced sales, or acquisition are not implied' was CONFIRMED still-live prior-P15 law as of the accepted 592e926 snapshot (`code-finance.md` §7, citing `P15-PACKAGE.md:619`). Direction F **SUPERSEDES** the 'loans' clause of that sentence only. The other four items — bailouts, investors, forced sales, acquisition — remain excluded from P15 exactly as written; this document's 'Rescue' loan is a priced, interest-bearing, repayable loan product, not a bailout, and does not draw on any of the four still-excluded categories. §16's surrounding 'at least two legitimate recovery routes / typed remedy capability family' framing is otherwise unaffected and is the correct authority for treating both loan products as P15B remedy capabilities."

## Finding 2 (MODERATE) — the §3.2 distress-interest surcharge is stated as a settled "Rule," then later (§8 item 4) admitted to need its own Owner sign-off

§3.2 opens: **"Rule:** once cash has been continuously negative for 8 consecutive weeks... a distress interest surcharge begins accruing..." — declarative, no hedge. Only in §8 ("Genuine Owner decisions this document leaves open"), item 4, does the document concede: "Whether the §3.2 distress-interest rule needs its own explicit Owner sign-off as a genuinely new mechanic (it is small, but it is new — negative cash currently costs nothing beyond the build lockout the original shipped, and this document adds a cost)."

This is not a violation of `P13-P15-OWNER-RULINGS.md` §6's "may not silently convert an open choice into package law," because the document *does* eventually flag it — but the placement (a firm "Rule:" in the design body, the caveat eight sections later, easy to miss on a partial read) creates exactly the kind of ambiguity that lens (a) is meant to catch. A builder who reads only §3.2–§3.7 (the part most directly actionable) would reasonably conclude the distress-interest charge is settled design, not an open Owner decision. It is also the one place in this design that changes existing, shipped player-facing behavior (negative cash currently costs nothing beyond the build lockout — `employment.ts:70-85`, confirmed correctly elsewhere in the document) rather than adding a new, previously-absent mechanic, which arguably makes it more Direction-adjacent (touches Law 5: "Negative cash is not bankruptcy. P11 cash and obligations inform distress **only through approved staged law**," `P15-PACKAGE.md` §11) than the document's own hedging suggests.

**Corrected statement:** State §3.2 as a **recommendation pending Owner sign-off** at first mention ("Recommended rule, not yet Owner-approved: ..."), not as a flat "Rule:", to avoid a builder treating it as settled ahead of the §8 disclosure.
