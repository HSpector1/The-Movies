4 · Icon silhouettes (one drawn set, filled two-tone, 16 / 20 / 28)

5 · Portrait standard (one system on every surface)

3/4 bust, role-coloured backplate (director teal, actor burgundy, writer olive, crew slate), rank badge slot top-left (illustrative here), status carried by the row chip and edge bar. Sizes: rail 44 × 53, marker 28, company faces 34, compact card 72, compare 96, dossier 120. Art dependency: twelve fixture portraits at these sizes (original illustration or licensed); the procedural placeholders shown are only a stand-in that proves distinguishability.

6 · Rail rows and statuses (normal · hover · selected · unavailable-with-reason)

Rail density and ordering: People — All / Talent / Writers / Crew tabs with counts; within a group, people needing a decision first, then on set / writing, then waiting, then free; search filters by name, role or ID. Pictures — grouped Scripts → Shooting → Post & release → Library, each group sorted with decisions first; "Needs me / Waiting / Library" tabs with counts. Overflow: each list scrolls independently with sticky group headers; long names clamp at two lines in the rail and appear in full in the inspector and on hover. Resizing: rails keep width at 1440/1920; at 1280 they narrow to 236/240 and the status chip wraps under the role. Selection feedback: gold outline + left bar on the row, gold site label and lit footprint on the lot, and the located marker — all from one state.

7 · Buttons, strips and response states

8 · Motion and audio intent (not implemented in the prototype)

Locate: the lot pans 350 ms ease-out to centre the target; the footprint outline fades in 150 ms; the site label switches to gold with no delay so keyboard users see the state immediately. Reduced-motion: the pan is instant.

Decision arrives: the "!" tab on a picture card scales 1 → 1.15 → 1 once (220 ms) and a short two-note cue plays (the manual's PA-system idea, not a voice line). Never loops; never blocks input.

Pending → receipt: the strip crossfades 150 ms; the primary button shows a 3-dot pending state; the receipt strip holds for 2 s then settles to the normal state. Refusal uses a single low tone.

Hover peek: appears after 180 ms, fades in 100 ms, closes on pointer departure. Right-click / "More facts" expands in place with height animation 180 ms.

Pause: the timeline marker stops pulsing and the PAUSED stamp appears; the world desaturates 8 %. No dimming of the rails — you still act while paused (as in the original).

Audio ownership: UI cues are short (≤ 300 ms), dry, and never stack more than two; world ambience continues under menus. Volume ducking of ambience by −6 dB while a workspace is open.
