## 1. Purpose and method

This matrix is the preservation contract for the autonomous P08–P10 stack. It maps every meaningful requirement retained from the three original research packages at decision-level granularity. It does not treat every historical observation as a feature. Each row preserves the original disposition and records where the requirement goes now.

Allowed present dispositions are: `IMPLEMENT IN CORE`, `IMPLEMENT AS READY EXTENSION`, `CONDITIONAL`, `OWNER-BLOCKED`, `DEPENDENCY-BLOCKED`, `DEFERRED TO NAMED PACKAGE`, `SUPERSEDED BY PROVED EQUIVALENT`, and `REJECTED BY OWNER`.

A core checkpoint is a floor, not an automatic stop ceiling. The coding lead may continue through READY EXTENSIONS only after the activation gate in the wave plan passes. Conditional, blocked, deferred, and rejected rows may not be improvised into implementation.
