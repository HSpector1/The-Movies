# Open and inspect the focused prototype

[Visual index](README.md). Extract the ZIP; open **index.html** in a desktop browser. All data is fictional, memory-only and disconnected. No dependencies or server are needed. The black top bar is a review harness outside the proposed game UI.

1. **Mature studio:** select The Glass Harbor on the right. Observe the camera-shaped card, gold selection, exact FILM-014, company and Stage 07. Inspect Nora Finch from the company row. Use Back or Escape to return to the same picture, then again to the same rail position. The duplicate Mara Vale names have separate roles and IDs.
2. **Waiting and context:** select After the Rain, then Inspect Production Post. Return to the exact waiting picture. It stays a wait: there is no “fix now” or estimated finish date. Inspect The Night Porter → Script Office → Back; Letters from June must not replace it merely because they share a site.
3. **Busy / narrow:** set Studio to Busy / long names. Scroll both rails. Open Ada Moss P-027 or a picture near the bottom; inspect/expand and Back. Both offsets and the invoking focus are retained. Use the right filter, Find by `BUSY-004`, paging arrows and Film library. At 1280×720 choose Enlarged and inspect the long titles and full status words. Lists intentionally scroll; an edge-partial row is not lost content.
4. **Candidate consistency:** The Long Way Home → Review casting draft → Inspect Leon → More facts. Leon's OVR is 83, fit 79 and picture fee $24,000; salary/term are unavailable. Back → inspect Celia: 76, 84, $18,000. Her proposed employment terms are distinct from the picture fee. Only Celia can be retained in the lead draft; nothing signs a contract.
5. **Response / same-view updates:** The Glass Harbor → Schedule shooting take. It shows pending then a simulated receipt; repeated submission is disabled. Choose Refusal in the harness before submission to exercise refusal and Review current state. Inspect a company member while waiting; the response remains attached to FILM-014 when you return. Reset resets the fictional fixture and clears any pending timer.
6. **Matched comparison:** change Right rail between Recommended hybrid and Closer to original. Data, world and viewport stay fixed. The closer treatment prioritizes a larger centered stage object but displays fewer complete cards. Review the full comparison in **comparisons.html**.
