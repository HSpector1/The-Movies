## P07 limitations carried forward

- wire result ID equals `productionId`;
- title is resolved display data, not a separately frozen FilmResult field;
- participants and forecasts exist only where captured;
- a missing requested result must not be replaced by fallback and called success;
- durable results do not prove every history launch route exists;
- windowed studio events are not all permanent;
- identity-reserving Tier D production IDs must survive retention/compaction;
- there is no universal external event-receipt contract;
- projected locked totals are not settled final public truth.

## Package-to-package activation gates

- P09 begins only after P08 core technical KEEP, migration proof, and pushed checkpoint.
- P10 begins only after P09 complete bare-lot first-film technical KEEP and pushed checkpoint.
- P08 facility/person extensions begin only after the exact P09/P10 producer facts exist.
- P08B/P08C and deeper P09/P10 systems remain outside this stack unless their separate requirements and decisions become satisfied.

## Future consumer boundary

Hollywood Wire may later consume typed factual history/results. Studio Radio remains downstream of Wire. Neither is a compile/runtime dependency of this stack. No runtime LLM creates facts.
