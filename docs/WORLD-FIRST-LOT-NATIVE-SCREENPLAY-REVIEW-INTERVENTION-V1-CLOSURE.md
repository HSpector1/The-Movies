- One strict current-review selector joins the exact Core decision, player-safe project card, and
  Core-emitted Accept/optional Rewrite actions. It rejects duplicate, sparse, extra-key, stale,
  malformed, or ambiguous authority instead of guessing or choosing another project.
- Newly surfaced event review and already-pending review retain distinct provenance. A current
  Core decision needs no synthetic receipt; a stale or malformed event receipt cannot downgrade
  into that path during the same gesture.
- The live Lot exposes exact title/ID, named writer, first/final state, visibly qualified `Est.`
  score and band, strengths, concerns, consequence, blockers, and exact legal actions in Core
  order. Hidden screenplay strength, skill, ceiling, and RNG inputs remain hidden.
- App captures and revalidates the complete rendered state/context/action, owns event consumption,
  dispatches `runScriptProjectAction` once, autosaves the exact successor, and repaints the same
  mounted Lot.
- Accept proves the same project Ready to package with no week/cash/capacity/RNG use. Rewrite proves
  the same project, writer, due week, Development & Casting facility, slot, and one-week consequence.
- Annex Working/selection appears only for an actual Annex reservation that the independent strict
  Annex projection also proves. A base slot remains semantic.
- Valid Engine successors survive presentation-validation failure. Rejection, stale identity,
  same-title projects, import/replacement, malformed session, deep return, and renderer failure
  preserve or clear only the authority they actually own and never substitute another review.
- Pointer, mouse, touch, keyboard, virtual-AT, repeat/cross-key tails, blur, cancel, visibility,
  modal, renderer readiness/recreation, and neighboring-button gestures are exact-once or neutral.
- The action-forward rail remains readable and operable at 960×540, effective 200%, maximum world
  zoom, and 480×270/DSF2, including forced colors, grayscale, reduced motion, focus scrolling, and
  one polite exact announcement.
- **Open Writers’ Room details** remains an optional supporting route after the world actions. Its
  return revalidates fresh current Lot truth and never restores a consumed receipt or cached card.
