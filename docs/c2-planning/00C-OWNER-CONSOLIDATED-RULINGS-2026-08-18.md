# OWNER RULING — C2 CONSOLIDATED RULINGS (2026-08-18, third ruling set)

Received during C2 advance planning, after charter r2. Fourteen numbered sections;
most CONFIRM rulings already recorded in `00A`/`00B` and bound in r2. Two are NEW and
change the charter materially; they are marked. "Architecture guardrails only — do
not expand C2 into rival studios, full Era progression, film-library economics, or
other future systems."

## Digest (what each section does to the charter)

1. **Living time — final experience requirement.** CONFIRMS `00A` (flows while
   unpaused; pause/speeds; advance-to-event demoted to convenience; deterministic
   engine authority; smallest implementation proving the hands-off sentence; Living
   Turn the preferred hypothesis, architecture selected on evidence). Already bound
   (charter §4.1, `08A`).
2. **Campaign horizon.** CONFIRMS `00A`/`00B`.10 (1920 start, no calendar game-over,
   ≥2040, alternate-future permitted; C2 avoids blocking architecture, builds no era
   content). Already bound (§1.10, G15).
3. **SCREENPLAY SUPPLY — NEW RULING, NEW C2 SCOPE.** *"THE CURRENT 30-CONCEPT POOL
   IS NOT THE LONG-TERM SCREENPLAY MODEL."* C2 establishes the smallest
   deterministic **Renewable Screenplay Generation V1**: writer works through a real
   development office; capacity is physical; player chooses creative direction
   (genre); writing consumes sim time and occupies writer + office; completion
   creates a **new permanent screenplay/concept identity** with a **generated
   working title**; the player may **rename** without changing the stable identity;
   no lifetime cap; more writers/offices = more throughput; queue-don't-forbid.
   The 30 pool concepts remain as seed data / templates / migration-compatible
   historical identities — never the lifetime supply. Existing `FilmConcept.id`
   values are permanent; new concepts append with fresh IDs. **C4 owns the deep
   version** (era-sensitive premises, evolving genre interest, research, cultural
   conditions). C2 owns the foundational fantasy: *"a writer goes to work and
   eventually hands me a new movie."* Placement (unified C2a vs bounded C2b slice)
   is the architect's to determine — but it may NOT be dropped to protect milestone
   size. **Evidence caveat preserved on Owner instruction:** the corpus
   independently confirms auto-generated scripts from writers assigned to Script
   Offices; it does NOT currently independently prove every detail of the Owner's
   remembered title-renaming interaction — the successor design is ruled regardless.
4. **Concurrency.** CONFIRMS owner laws 1–2 (physical capacity; queue-don't-forbid;
   the four-facts-plus-remedy legibility). Adds: exact slot counts are the
   architect's to recommend; **do not replace the global cap with another arbitrary
   global cap** (already the design — capacity-derived admission, §3.3; the agent
   policy constant bounds agents, not the studio).
5. **Sets and Stages.** CONFIRMS laws 3/4/5. Already bound (§3).
6. **Simulation theater.** CONFIRMS law 8, adds the standing phrase **"EVERYTHING
   BELONGS TO A SYSTEM"** — visible activity answers "why is that person/object
   there?". Already bound (§4.2 Class-A/B discipline, grounded ambient actors).
7. **THEATER RULING — NEW; REVERSES the r2 recommendation.** The Theater is **not
   core release authority**; release authority belongs to the production/
   distribution workflow. A Screening Theater / Premiere House may exist LATER as
   optional player-built prestige/test-screening/festival infrastructure or a
   historical landmark. **Not required to make Movie #1; the Founding Flip does NOT
   seed one; Premiere Night V1 must function regardless of theater ownership; an
   external/city premiere destination is acceptable. Do not delete the Theater
   concept; remove it as a required mechanical release dependency.** (r2 had
   recommended "permanent landmark, included in the Flip minimum lot" — that
   recommendation is withdrawn; the master plan §6 minimum-lot sentence stands as
   originally written: Gate + Administration + road + parcels.)
8. **Premiere Night V1.** CONFIRMS C2 ownership and the tasteful-visible-event
   target (marquee, searchlights where appropriate, crowd, photographers, star
   arrivals, publicity, handoff to newspaper/results); no footage; presentation
   reacting to authoritative release truth. Venue design must satisfy ruling 7.
9. **Founding Flip.** CONFIRMS ratification and the `INITIAL_PROPERTY` immutability
   guardrail (`00B`.1) verbatim — never edit the V12→V13 anchor; a separate named
   bare-start property definition; historical saves keep reconstructing against the
   original anchor. Already bound (§7.1).
10. **Future-proofing guardrails A–G.** CONFIRMS `00B` (production identity;
    frozen leaves; talent census; ledger history; studio-relative facts;
    authoritative time) and adds **G — RNG discipline stated explicitly**: new
    systems never consume the existing persisted sim stream merely because it is
    available; purpose-keyed versioned deterministic streams (the `presence-v1`
    precedent). Binds RSG title generation directly.
11. **Long-campaign performance — aware, not derailed.** New systems avoid obvious
    whole-history scans and unbounded per-frame work; never delete history to go
    fast; optimize on evidence. Recorded as an engine note (§8.2).
12. **C2 success experience.** The full chain, now including the RSG head: build
    office → assign writer → new screenplay over time → new identity/title →
    review → casting → rehearsal → set/stage reservation → visible production →
    queues on conflict → shooting → wrap → release → premiere → results → the next
    picture already moving — *"the world continues operating unless I pause it."*
13. **Explicit non-goals.** CONFIRMS and extends the r2 list (rival studios,
    acquisitions, subsidiaries, bankruptcy, awards season, film-library economics,
    sequels/franchises/remakes, full era progression, 1920–2040 content catalog,
    cultural drift, star life, deep relationships, machinima, portrait/art
    campaign, final soundtrack, full economy rebalance).
14. **Charter expectation.** The charter answers fourteen named questions; §14 of
    this ruling maps onto the charter's structure (r3 adds the RSG and revised
    Theater/Premiere answers).
