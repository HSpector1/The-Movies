## 1. Repair §2 — world-person selection and occlusion

### 1.1 Root cause (product)

`StudioSelectionManager.SightLineBlocked` decided that an occluder collider was "the ground the person
stands on" whenever the collider's axis-aligned bounds CONTAINED the person's feet or chest, and then
waived it. For an authored building whose physical box spans the whole footprint (the Production &amp;
Post building: `Post Body` 22×7.6×18 on the Default layer, exactly the rendered mass) that also waived
the building for a person at the BACK of the box, fully hidden behind the facade — the independent
review's MAJOR finding. Being inside a footprint is not evidence of being visible.

The premise behind that rule was itself wrong. The real-input body probe of 2026-09-06 08:46
(`Evidence/P10-Body-Probe-CloseGates/hid-20260906T084618Z`, sample 2) was read as "a person on the
Post's doorstep, drawn in front of the facade, whose click resolved `post`". The probe's own numbers
place that person at world (−5.17, 1.25, 48.26): 4.3 m NORTH of the Post Body's back face (z = 44),
under its 7.6 m mass, from a camera looking north — i.e. genuinely hidden behind the building
(screenshot `003-probe-2.png`, aim point on the Post's roof edge). The `post` pick was correct; the
"doorstep" exemption was built to defeat a correct answer and, as the reviewer found, defeated the
rule everywhere else too. The scene parse confirms no authored building on this lot has a collider
coarser than its rendered mass, so no real doorstep person was ever inside a collider.

**Placed P09 facilities were worse: they never occluded anyone.** Every rendered part of a placed
body (`StudioLotPlacedBody.CreateKit`) is a primitive with its collider stripped, on the selection
layer (8); the body's only collider is the root selection box, also on layer 8 — which
`OcclusionMask` deliberately excludes. A person standing behind a finished placed building was hidden
visually and selectable straight through it. Same general defect, second owner.

### 1.2 The correction (Unity `c71ffff`)

- **Sight-line law.** A person is occluded by any opaque collider the exact ray meets BEFORE it meets
  the person's own body surface. No containment or porch exemption. The target distance is the ray's
  hit on the person's collider (the body surface nearest the camera), so a person genuinely standing in
  a doorway — whose body protrudes past the envelope's face — is met by the ray BEFORE the envelope, and
  the envelope is never "ahead" of them. A body the ray can only reach by passing through an opaque
  collider is behind it: a thin wall in front, a mass they stand inside, a roof above. The visible facade
  (or the next visible target) takes the click. `PickWithPeopleTolerance` (the 8-px ring) resolves each
  ring ray through the same law, so a hidden neighbour never steals a near miss.
- **Placed facilities occlude what they draw.** Each placed body now carries a `__Occluder` child —
  a bare Default-layer `BoxCollider` — sized in `Layout()` to mirror exactly what is drawn opaque: the
  finished mass once operational (`massX × height × massZ`), the rising walls from the enclosure band
  (`massX × 0.6·height × massZ`), and nothing before that (an open frame and hoarding hide nobody). The
  root selection envelope is unchanged, so the facility itself stays selectable exactly as before.
- **Unchanged and preserved:** exact-id selection, decorative-person boundaries (`SetSemanticKind`),
  Person-over-Place ranking, the ring tolerance, nameplate/marker targets, the Gate applicant law.
