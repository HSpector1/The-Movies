The reviewed capacity settings are **192 MiB decoded outer checkpoint, 64 MiB replay journal, 512 journal entries, 32 records, 256 MiB encoded library, 1 GiB aggregate decoded checkpoints**. Keep these as fail-closed ceilings, not target operating sizes or a promise that 32 maximum-sized records fit simultaneously.

Rationale: one exact accepted Save receipt measured 49,595,194 bytes, exceeding the previous 16 MiB journal limit. The accepted receipt plus later working state measured 134,753,428 bytes, exceeding the previous 128 MiB checkpoint limit. Raising both bounds together preserves historical idempotency receipts and prevents immediate rollover churn. The continuation successfully proved exact Save/retry, restart after later progress, inactive-record preservation, controlled rollover, failed Save rollback, and loading all three records. Its maximum acknowledged encoded library was 71,180,676 bytes; final size was 63,025,258 bytes. The records were independent Save As records of one synthetic seed, not three native New Game worlds.

Storage format2 gzip/base64 is lossless representation: exact outer1 text, both slots and receipts are retained; canonical inner V19 and identity/history remain authoritative. Existing checksum, exact-base64, decoded-size, UTF-8 and aggregate limits remain necessary. The encoded cache must enforce each caller's bound. Tradeoffs include base64 expansion, several copies during parse/validation/encoding, CPU-heavy synchronous portions, and whole-library atomic writes. A 1 GiB decode ceiling does not imply 1 GiB peak memory. The default-flags Node probe sampled 4.999 GB RSS on an Apple M3 Max with128 GiB RAM, including probe data; this is neither packaged-app RSS nor a hardware-minimum acceptance result.

The bounded 60-second mutation/startup and90-second runner Quit allowances prevent premature failure of the measured workload. They are recovery ceilings, not replacement UX targets. Exact pending request bytes, refreshed authority joins, and Quit only after its own matching durable receipt remain mandatory. The lazy fork removes demonstrably redundant work; its end-to-end improvement still needs measurement.
