# Project: Studio — Roadmap

```
M0A  →  M1A  →  M0B  →  V1
```

Each stage is gated. Nothing starts until the one before it reports back.

## ▶ M0A — headless film harness  *(current)*

**Contract:** `studio/docs/build-contract.md`, phases 1–4
**Deliverable:** instrumentation report over ~1,000 seeded runs + acceptance test results
**Answers:** do the film-assembly maths produce real decisions, or is one strategy dominant?

The agent runs this loop autonomously — implement, run, read flags, tune `TUNING`, re-run.

Two results to watch:
- **Four-quadrant test** — "uniform but dull" must exist as a distinct outcome. If it can't,
  cohesion is swallowing craft and every coherent film is automatically good.
- **Standing differentiation** — fewer than 3 of 4 asymmetric profiles means the three
  reputation channels collapsed back into one master stat by accident.

## M1A — playable film laboratory

**Contract:** same file, phases 5–6. **Requires your explicit approval to start.**
**Deliverable:** UI over the proven engine. One year, no aging, no economy.
Conceive → shape → promise → cast → budget → release → autopsy. Plus the talent
creator and the Broadcast.

**Gate, and it's the real one:** do you want to open it again next weekend without
deciding to? Also — do you talk about a finished film as a *work* ("my comedy worked
with families but the lead was too severe") or a *number* ("that one got 74")? The
second means Vision didn't make films into authored objects.

If M1A isn't fun, the project stops here. That's what the gate is for.

## M0B — career & talent market harness

**Contract:** `design-archive/next-contracts/1-career-talent-market.md`
Headless again — careers, aging, retirement, contracts, poaching, over 30 years.

Internal order: craft progression and age → fame and prestige → retirement and intake →
contracts and rival ownership → two-way poaching → actor-to-director transitions
(flagged, last).

**Answers:** does developing talent create real decisions, and can you lose people you built?

## V1 — the full game

The proven film loop plus progression and the talent market, then:

1. `2-historical-talent.md` — development scarcity, historical seeds as prospects.
   *Key test: run the same century twenty times. The legends should differ.*
2. `3-acquisition.md` — in three gated steps: discovery and evaluation first
   (smallest, highest value), then offers, then loan-outs and reputation.

## Not in V1

Chemistry · readable memories · production incidents · the lot · awards season · rival
studios resolving their own films · scene composition · screenplay generation · visual
film output · library economics · receivership · LLM-written Broadcast copy

Several have design work behind them. That's not permission — each was cut on purpose.

## Deferred but wanted

- **The full 1927–2065 span** with era rule modules and generated futures. `design-spec.md` §3.
- **Broadcast with generated prose.** Templates first, always.
- **Watching the films.** Text and storyboards long before anything animated.

## Three standing rules

Settled, and all three have been accidentally violated at least once:

1. **Persona distance means execution fit. Nothing else.** Any system wanting a
   "how appropriate is this pairing" number gets its own variable.
2. **Generation narrates, never decides.** No player-authored free text ever reaches
   a scoring function.
3. **One contract reaches the agent at a time.** The current one, alone, in the repo.
